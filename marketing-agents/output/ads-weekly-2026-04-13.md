**Ticketmaster:**
- Dominant organic presence (they own the inventory)
- Paid ads focus on "Verified Tickets" and "Official Source" messaging
- Running "Verified Fan" presale campaigns for summer concerts
- **Gap we can exploit:** "Official" doesn't mean cheapest. Our data shows Ticketmaster is cheapest only 22% of the time. The "Verified Fan" angle is about access, not price — different value prop entirely.

**Vivid Seats:**
- Light paid presence, mostly brand defense
- "Reward Program" angle — loyalty points for purchases
- Not actively competing on comparison or price terms
