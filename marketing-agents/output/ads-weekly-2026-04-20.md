Week 1 recommended Playoff Price Gap as the new-campaign launch. Playoff campaigns remain valuable but face two structural problems right now:
1. **Past-event filter is unshipped** — broad playoff keyword campaigns will burn budget on post-game queries. Single-matchup ad groups become liabilities within hours of puck drop / tip-off.
2. **Venue-page refreshes are undeployed (Day 3+)** — Ball Arena, American Airlines Center, and (scheduled) Chase Center venue pages all have working-tree edits. Routing team-specific paid traffic to `/compare` works, but venue-routing would be higher-contextual-match once the deploy lands.

Comparison Head Terms is **evergreen, LP-ready, and Quality-Score-maximizing**. It's the Week 3 launch that doesn't depend on any unship, doesn't have same-day expiration risk, and is optimally positioned to take advantage of the Apr 17 `/compare` schema work once it deploys.
