# Campaign Draft — Search-Venue-Event-Discovery-US-2026-10

**Status:** Launch-ready draft; not launched.  
**Platform:** Google Search  
**Objective:** qualified event discovery and outbound seller clicks  
**Landing page:** matching event page or verified venue guide; `/dashboard` only for broad discovery terms

## Hypothesis

Specific event, venue, and onsale/presale searches should produce more qualified traffic than broad deal language because the destination can answer the exact event and practical buyer questions.

## Audience and structure

- **Event discovery:** `[artist/team] tickets`, `[event] tickets`, `[event] schedule`
- **Venue intent:** `[venue] tickets`, `[venue] seating guide`, `[venue] events`
- **Onsale/presale:** `[event] presale`, `[artist] onsale time`, `[venue] presale`

Use phrase and exact match initially. Add negatives only after reviewing actual search terms. Do not exclude “cheap tickets,” “ticket deals,” or competitor terms without query-level evidence and campaign-scope review.

## Budget and test window

No budget was authorized or entered. The account owner must set a daily cap and target CPA before activation. Run for seven days, with a minimum three clean attributed days before judgment.

## Measurement

- Primary: `outbound_ticket_click`
- Secondary: `signup_completed`, `watchlist_added`, `newsletter_subscribe`
- Required UTMs: `utm_source=google`, `utm_medium=cpc`, `utm_campaign=venue-event-discovery-us-2026-10`, unique `utm_content`
- Winner: approved CPA target met with qualified downstream-action rate at least as good as control
