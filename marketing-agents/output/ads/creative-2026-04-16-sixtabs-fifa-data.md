### Variation C — NHL Single-Series $93-to-$224 Price Spread

**Google Search Ad:**
```
Headline 1: $93 in Dallas. $224 in Minnesota.
Headline 2: Same NHL Playoff Series, Wild Spread
Headline 3: Compare All 3 Platforms Free
Description 1: Stars vs Wild Game 1 in Dallas tonight: $93 gets you in. Game 3 in Minnesota: $171. Game 6: $224. Same first-round series. Different cities, different platforms, different prices.
Description 2: Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, and StubHub price every playoff game differently. One search shows all three so you find the floor, not just the first tab you opened.
```
- **Final URL:** https://www.ticketscan.io/compare
- **Display path:** ticketscan.io/compare/nhl-playoffs
- **Sitelinks:** Price Alerts, How It Works, World Cup 2026, Venue Guides

**Meta Ad:**
```
Primary text: Stars vs Wild Game 1 is tonight in Dallas. Floor price: $93. Same series, Game 3 in Minnesota, jumps to $171. If it goes to Game 6 — and first-round NHL usually does — you're looking at $224. Eighty-plus bucks apart because Minnesota is the road trip nobody plans for. That's one series on one platform. StubHub, SeatGeek, and Ticketmaster each price these games differently on top of the venue and game-number swings. One search on TicketScan shows all three for whichever game you're targeting.
Headline: $93 or $224? Same Series.
Description: Compare playoff tickets — free.
```
- **CTA button:** Learn More
- **Landing:** https://www.ticketscan.io/compare
- **Audience:** NHL fans 21-50, interest in NHL / Stanley Cup Playoffs / Dallas Stars / Minnesota Wild, metros: Dallas-Fort Worth, Minneapolis-St. Paul, Denver, Edmonton, Winnipeg, Tampa, Ottawa, Toronto
- **Note:** Multi-week shelf life through the first and second rounds. Update game numbers and prices every 2-3 days as the series progresses. Adaptable template: swap team names for any series with a home/road price delta (e.g., Knicks-Celtics, Lakers-Warriors, whichever Round 1 matchups lock Sunday).

**Why this angle:** NHL playoff pricing has a structural feature that other sports don't showcase as cleanly: the same series crosses multiple cities with dramatically different price floors, and each platform prices those games independently. The $93→$224 range within a single first-round series is an immediately striking concrete number. Social is running Stars-Wild price gaps at 1 PM ET today (Twitter). This angle sustains through the entire 6-8 week NHL playoff run with rotating team/price data. Low ad-copy decay rate because the template is the point — we just swap in new series as they advance.
