```
Description 2: We track World Cup 2026 pricing daily across every host city. Free comparison. 54 days until the opener at Estadio Azteca.
```
- **Final URL:** https://www.ticketscan.io/world-cup-2026
- **Display path:** ticketscan.io/world-cup-2026
- **Sitelinks:** All 16 Stadiums, Group Stage Tickets, How It Works, Price Alerts
- **Match types:** Exact on "world cup 2026 tickets," "world cup resale tickets," "[stadium name] world cup tickets"; phrase on "fifa 2026 tickets compare."
- **Geo:** All 11 US + 2 Canada + 3 Mexico host metros. Broad national secondary tier for national search.

**Meta Ad:**
```
Primary text: 54 days out from the World Cup 2026 opener at Estadio Azteca. Group-stage resale across all sixteen host venues ran 8.2% higher this week than the week before. That's not a spike — that's a steady weekly climb. Every week you wait to buy, the gap between primary seller prices and resale widens another notch. We track every match across Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, and StubHub daily. One search, every platform, every venue. Free to compare. The tournament isn't getting cheaper.
Headline: 54 Days. Resale Up 8.2%.
Description: Compare before the next move.
```
- **CTA button:** Learn More
- **Landing:** https://www.ticketscan.io/world-cup-2026
- **Audience:** Soccer/football fans 25-55, interest in FIFA / World Cup / MLS / Premier League / La Liga, US + Canada + Mexico (English + Spanish creative variants), travel intent signal (broad), exclude frequent flyer targeting (budget-conscious creative).
- **Note:** First World Cup creative since Apr 16 that isn't time-boxed to FIFA headline cycles. The 8.2% WoW figure is *data urgency* — different from a static countdown. Pairs with today's Twitter #3 (5pm ET). Next WC creative refresh beat: Apr 22 (FIFA resale 2-week blog data).

**Why this angle:** Raw countdowns (54 days) are content — they don't move anyone to buy today. Raw percentages (+8.2%) without context are finance-bro noise. The combination — specific near-term date plus specific weekly climb — reframes the decision as "delay costs me 8% per week." That's a behavioral nudge, not a passive number. Shelf life: one week per data refresh. The Content Agent's Apr 22 FIFA resale 2-week blog will deliver the next refresh input naturally. Best suited for always-on cold audiences once launch clears — evergreen structure, rotating data.
