## Variation I — STRENGTHEN ("No Follow-up, No Retargeting" Copy Framework)

**Status:** **STRENGTHENED Day 8 of generalized empirical-foundation carry.** The 48h continuous null-add watchlist window seals the most decisive single-day empirical evidence in the 39-day carry. Combined retention sample is n=27 = 0% across 4 day-thresholds; burst + mini-burst + single-add all fail. **The hypothesis underpinning Variation I — "high-activity Day-0 behavior is NOT a leading indicator of Day-1+ return; retention failure is structural" — has its strongest confirmation to date.**

**Why this matters for copy:** Variation I's voice is the consumer-advocate-not-stalker positioning. Most ticket platforms retarget aggressively (cookie-pixel chase across the web, email cadences, push notifications). The TicketScan-Variation-I claim is: **"We don't follow up. You came, you compared, you bought or you didn't — that's the whole loop."** The 27-of-27 retention seal is the empirical foundation: by the platform's own measure, users don't return; copy that pretends they will is dishonest. Variation I aligns the copy with the data.

- Geo: National (no geo-narrows; behavioral skew on retargeting-aversion proxies)
- Behavioral: Brave-browser users, ad-blocker users, "tracking" / "privacy" interest narrows (Meta) / `compare ticket prices`, `ticket prices side by side`, `no email signup ticket comparison`, `quick ticket price check` (Google exact + phrase match)
- Negative-list reinforcement: `track ticket prices` / `price alerts email` / `price drop email alert` (these stay PERMANENT NEGATIVE — they contradict the Variation I copy claim)
