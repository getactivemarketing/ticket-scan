Variation I's "no follow-up, no retargeting" copy positioning remains intact at the longitudinal measure but can now acknowledge the single-day demand signal without compromising the core claim.

**Why the reframe matters for copy:** The temptation after a +9 burst is to add demand-side framing ("X new users signed up today" / "9 watchlists added in 24h") — but this risks four problems: (1) it contradicts the "we don't watch you" privacy positioning; (2) it reads as engagement-FOMO copy, which is the inverse of the consumer-advocate voice; (3) it overstates the empirical claim (the burst is a single-day measure, not a structural pattern); (4) it commits Variation I to a retention-claim that's still 0% at longitudinal measure. **The reframe holds the core copy and adds a Day-40-only demand-side hold variant to test the boundary**: same single-session promise, with a confidence-anchor that hints at "people are using this" without making it the lead.

**Audience targeting:** Unchanged from Day 39. Privacy-aware buyer cluster (Brave browser, ad-blocker users, Meta privacy-interest narrows); Google exact + phrase match on `compare ticket prices`, `ticket prices side by side`, `no email signup ticket comparison`, `quick ticket price check`.
