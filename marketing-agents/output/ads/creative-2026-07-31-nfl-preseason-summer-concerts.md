# Ad Creative — 2026-07-31 — NFL Preseason, Concerts, and Price Alerts

All claims avoid unverified savings or user-count figures. Recommended launch order: Variation 1, then Variation 2, then Variation 3 after event/category landing-page tracking is confirmed.

## Variation 1 — Evergreen Price Alerts

**Angle:** Buy at the price you want, not the price the market happens to show today.

**Google Search Ad**

```text
Headline 1 (30): Ticket Price Drop Alerts
Headline 2 (30): Compare 3 Ticket Sources
Headline 3 (30): Set Your Target Price
Description 1 (90): TicketScan compares Ticketmaster, SeatGeek and StubHub in one place.
Description 2 (90): Set a target and get alerted when prices move down. Free to start.
```

**Meta Ad**

```text
Primary text (125): Ticket prices move. Your budget does not. Compare major ticket sites and get an alert when your target price hits.
Headline (40): Get Ticket Price Drop Alerts
Description (30): Compare first. Buy smarter.
```

**Landing page:** `/dashboard` for search-led traffic; homepage only if the alert CTA is prominent.

## Variation 2 — NFL Preseason

**Angle:** Early-season football intent without claiming a specific price or discount.

**Google Search Ad**

```text
Headline 1 (30): Compare NFL Ticket Prices
Headline 2 (30): Ticketmaster vs SeatGeek
Headline 3 (30): Find Your Best Seat Deal
Description 1 (90): Compare NFL tickets across major sources before preseason demand picks up.
Description 2 (90): Check prices side by side, then track your team's market for a better buy.
```

**Meta Ad**

```text
Primary text (125): Football is back on the calendar. Before you buy your team's tickets, check what every major source is charging.
Headline (40): Compare NFL Tickets Before Buying
Description (30): One search. Multiple sources.
```

**Landing page:** `/tickets/nfl-tickets` if the route is live; otherwise `/dashboard`.

## Variation 3 — Summer Concerts / Event-Day Intent

**Angle:** Event-day prices can vary; comparison reduces tab-hopping and guesswork.

**Google Search Ad**

```text
Headline 1 (30): Compare Concert Ticket Prices
Headline 2 (30): See Major Sources Together
Headline 3 (30): Check Before You Buy
Description 1 (90): Stop opening four ticket tabs. See comparable listings in one TicketScan search.
Description 2 (90): Track the event and watch the price trend before you commit to a seat.
```

**Meta Ad**

```text
Primary text (125): Same event, different sites, very different prices. TicketScan puts the comparison in one place before you check out.
Headline (40): Stop Opening Four Ticket Tabs
Description (30): See the market first.
```

**Landing page:** `/dashboard`.

## Test notes

- Add UTMs by platform, campaign, angle, and landing page.
- Primary conversion: `search_initiated` initially; optimize to `watchlist_added` only after the event is firing reliably.
- Do not use “save $X,” “X,000 users,” or “lowest price” language until the claim is backed by a live, queryable report.
