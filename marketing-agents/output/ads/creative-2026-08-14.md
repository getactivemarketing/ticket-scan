## Test notes

- Use distinct UTMs for each variation: `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`.
- Test one angle at a time for at least three days or 100 clicks after instrumentation is live.
- Avoid specific savings, live-price, inventory, social-proof, or alert-volume claims until verified.
