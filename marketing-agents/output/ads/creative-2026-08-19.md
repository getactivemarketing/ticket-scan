Three test-ready variations. Claims are intentionally product-led; no unverified savings, price, or user-count claims are used.

### Test notes

- Primary hypothesis: naming all three sources and the comparison action will improve qualified click-through versus generic “find tickets” copy.
- Destination: `/compare` for Variations 1–2; `/world-cup-2026/` for Variation 3.
- Do not publish “save $X,” “from $X,” or social-proof claims until those values are verified in the ad account and current product data.
