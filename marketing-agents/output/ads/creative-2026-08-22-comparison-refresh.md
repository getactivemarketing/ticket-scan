## Test Notes

- Primary hypothesis: comparison-first language will produce higher-quality visits than generic “cheap tickets” language.
- Test one variation per ad set against the current control; do not change landing page and creative simultaneously.
- Track `landing_cta_click`, `search_submit`, `compare_started`, `compare_completed`, and `outbound_ticket_click` before judging CPA.
- Do not add fabricated savings, live price movement, alert delivery, or user-count claims until those data sources are verified.
