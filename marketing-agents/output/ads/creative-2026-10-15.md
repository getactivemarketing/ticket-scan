# Paid Ad Creative — 2026-10-15

**Status:** Drafts for testing. No live campaign or top performer was available.

These variations avoid unsupported claims about price tracking, price history, price drops, alerts, buy timing, savings, or TicketScan-derived statistics.

## Variation 1 — Event discovery

**Angle:** Find the event, then choose where to buy.

### Google Search

- **Headline 1:** Find Tickets in One Place
- **Headline 2:** Compare Your Buying Options
- **Headline 3:** Search TicketScan Today
- **Description 1:** Find upcoming games, concerts, and shows with clear event details and buying links.
- **Description 2:** Check onsale dates, venue details, and your options before you commit.

### Meta

- **Primary text:** Ticket hunting should not feel like opening 14 tabs. Find the event, check the details, and see where to buy.
- **Headline:** Find Your Next Event
- **Description:** Search smarter. Buy informed.

**Suggested destination:** Homepage or the most relevant city/category page.

## Variation 2 — Consumer advocate

**Angle:** Make the total-ticket decision with fewer surprises.

### Google Search

- **Headline 1:** Check Before You Buy
- **Headline 2:** See Ticket Options Clearly
- **Headline 3:** Start Your Ticket Search
- **Description 1:** Find the event and review buying options before checkout. A little homework beats fee roulette.
- **Description 2:** Use venue tips and check the all-in total before paying for your seats.

### Meta

- **Primary text:** The ticket price is only part of the story. Find your event, check your options, and look at the all-in total before checkout.
- **Headline:** Be A Smarter Ticket Buyer
- **Description:** Fewer surprises at checkout.

**Suggested destination:** `/compare` only after confirming the page accurately describes its current outbound-buying flow; otherwise use the homepage.

- **Description 1:** Explore World Cup 2026 match details, host venues, seating guides, and ticket-buying information.

**Suggested destination:** `/world-cup-2026/`.

## QA before launch

- Confirm headline and description character limits in the ad platform.
- Append campaign, ad, and keyword UTMs to every destination.
- Confirm all claims remain true on the destination page.
- Do not add price-drop, alert, price-history, buy/wait/hold, average-savings, or TicketScan-data claims until the product-status restriction is lifted.
