# Paid Ad Creative — 2026-10-16

**Status:** Drafts for testing. No live top-performing campaign was available,
so these are three independent angles rather than a claimed winner.

All copy stays on supported claims: event discovery, onsale/presale timing,
venue context, and links to ticket sellers. It avoids price tracking, price
history, price drops, alerts, buy/wait calls, savings statistics, and TicketScan
price-data claims.

## Variation 1 — Event discovery

**Angle:** Find the event without the tab explosion.

### Google Search

- **Headline 1:** Find Events, Skip the Hunt
- **Headline 2:** See Where to Buy Tickets
- **Headline 3:** Search TicketScan Today
- **Description 1:** Find upcoming games, concerts, and shows with event details and buying links.
- **Description 2:** Check the date, venue, and onsale timing before you commit.

### Meta

- **Primary text:** Ticket hunting should not require 14 tabs. Find the event, check the details, and see where to buy.
- **Headline:** Find Your Next Event
- **Description:** Search smarter. Buy informed.

**Suggested destination:** `/dashboard` or the most relevant event/city page.

## Variation 2 — Consumer advocate

**Angle:** Fewer surprises at checkout.

### Google Search

- **Headline 1:** Check Before You Buy
- **Headline 2:** Review Ticket Options Clearly
- **Headline 3:** Start Your Ticket Search
- **Description 1:** Find the event and review buying options before checkout. Fee roulette is a bad game.
- **Description 2:** Use venue tips, then confirm fees and the all-in total on the seller site.

### Meta

- **Primary text:** The listed ticket is only part of the story. Check the event, venue, delivery, fees, and final total before paying.
- **Headline:** Be a Smarter Buyer
- **Description:** Fewer checkout surprises.

**Suggested destination:** `/dashboard` or a relevant venue guide.

## Variation 3 — World Cup 2026

**Angle:** Tournament discovery plus venue planning.

### Google Search

- **Headline 1:** World Cup 2026 Tickets
- **Headline 2:** Explore Matches and Venues
- **Headline 3:** Plan Your Match Search
- **Description 1:** Explore World Cup 2026 match details, host venues, seating guides, and buying information.
- **Description 2:** Get dates and venue context first, then follow available ticket links.

### Meta

- **Primary text:** Planning for World Cup 2026? Explore match details, host venues, seating guides, and ticket-buying information.
- **Headline:** Plan Your World Cup Search
- **Description:** Match and venue context.

**Suggested destination:** `/world-cup-2026/`.

## QA before launch

- Confirm headline and description character limits in the ad platform.
- Append campaign, ad, and keyword UTMs to every destination.
- Confirm every claim on the destination page, especially event dates and
  onsale/presale timing.
- Do not launch price-drop, alert, price-history, buy/wait/hold, savings, or
  TicketScan-derived price claims until the product-status restriction is lifted.
