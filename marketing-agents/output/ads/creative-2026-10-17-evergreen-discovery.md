# Paid Creative Drafts — 2026-10-17

Three testable variations for an evergreen event-discovery campaign. These drafts intentionally avoid unsupported claims about price tracking, price history, price drops, alerts, savings, or buy/wait recommendations.

## Variation 1 — Listing discovery

**Angle:** Find the event, then review seller options.

### Google Search

- Headline 1: `Find Tickets in One Place` (27/30)
- Headline 2: `Ticketmaster & More` (20/30)
- Headline 3: `Search Your Next Event` (23/30)
- Description 1: `Find concerts and sports events, then review available seller links in one place.` (82/90)
- Description 2: `Search by event, city, and date. Check the seller's final availability and fees.` (80/90)

### Meta

- Primary text: `The ticket hunt is already chaotic. Find your event, see where to buy, and skip the tab avalanche.` (101/125)
- Headline: `Find Your Next Event` (21/40)
- Description: `Search. Compare listings.` (25/30)

## Variation 2 — Consumer advocate

**Angle:** Make the checkout comparison habit explicit without promising a TicketScan price result.

## Variation 3 — Venue guide utility

**Angle:** Use verified venue information as the differentiator.
