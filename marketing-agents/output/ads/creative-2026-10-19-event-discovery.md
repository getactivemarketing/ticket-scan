# Paid Creative Refresh — 2026-10-19

Launch-ready copy for the proposed **Event Discovery — Onsale & Venue Intent**
test. None of these variations has been uploaded. All copy avoids unsupported
price tracking, alerts, buy/wait calls, savings claims, and TicketScan price
statistics.

## Google Search responsive-search-ad variations

Use one variation per ad group so the test changes the angle without changing
audience and landing page at the same time. Headlines are written to fit the
usual 30-character RSA limit; confirm final character counts in the account.

### Variation 1 — Find the event

- Headlines: `Find Tickets for Your Event` · `See Where to Buy` · `TicketScan Event Search`
- Descriptions: `Find upcoming sports, concerts, and shows, then follow links to the seller.` · `Check event details, venue information, and onsale timing in one place.`
- CTA: **Find events**

### Variation 2 — Onsale timing

- Headlines: `Know When Tickets Go On Sale` · `Track Onsale & Presale Times` · `Plan Your Ticket Search`
- Descriptions: `Check verified onsale and presale timing before you start your ticket search.` · `Find the event, review the venue, and continue to the available seller.`
- CTA: **See onsale dates**

### Variation 3 — Venue confidence

- Headlines: `Your Venue Ticket Guide` · `Explore Seating Sections` · `Know the Venue Before You Go`
- Descriptions: `Review seating sections, capacity, and practical venue tips before buying.` · `Find events at the venue and follow the link to purchase from the seller.`
- CTA: **Explore venue guide**

### Variation 4 — Buyer checklist

- Headlines: `A Smarter Ticket Search` · `Check Fees Before Checkout` · `Find Tickets With Context`
- Descriptions: `Find events and compare the details that matter before visiting a seller site.` · `Confirm fees, delivery, section, and the final total at checkout.`
- CTA: **Start your search**

### Variation 5 — World Cup 2026

- Headlines: `World Cup 2026 Ticket Guide` · `Explore World Cup Venues` · `Find Match Information`
- Descriptions: `Explore 2026 World Cup venues, match information, and ticket-buying paths.` · `Review stadium details and follow the available seller link for the next step.`
- CTA: **Explore World Cup**

## Meta primary text + headline pairs

- Primary text: `Looking for your next game, concert, or show? TicketScan helps you find the event, review the details, and see where to buy.`
- Headline: `Find events. See where to buy.`
- CTA: **Learn More**

- Headline: `Know the venue before you go`

## Display/social image concepts

### Concept A — “One place to start”

- Visual: Three-card composition: event, venue, seller link.
- Overlay: `Find the event. Know the venue. See where to buy.`
- Supporting line: `TicketScan event discovery`
- Destination: `/dashboard`
- Guardrail: no prices, savings, alerts, or “best time” language.

### Concept B — “Onsale calendar”

- Visual: Calendar showing `ONSALE` and `PRESALE` labels with a neutral event card.
- Overlay: `Know when tickets go on sale`
- Supporting line: `Check timing, then follow the seller link.`
- Destination: `/onsales`
- Guardrail: use only dates/times verified by the page data; no invented dates.

## Testing plan

1. Start with Variation 1 as control and test Variation 2 as the only copy
   change. Keep audience, bid strategy, budget, and destination constant.
2. Run for at least 3 days or 100 clicks before judging, provided tracking is
   working. Record CTR, qualified landing-page sessions, search submits,
   outbound seller clicks, signups, and cost per verified conversion.
3. If the destination or conversion event is not measurable, stop the test;
   do not select a winner from CTR alone.
4. Pass the winner to CRO only with the ad ID, landing page, UTM values, and
   observed conversion definition.
