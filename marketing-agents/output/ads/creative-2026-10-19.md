# Paid Ad Creative — 2026-10-19

Three evergreen discovery variations for review once measurement and ad
accounts are connected. Copy avoids unsupported claims about price tracking,
price history, price drops, alerts, buy/wait recommendations, savings, or
TicketScan-generated price data.

## Variation 1 — Find the event

**Angle:** Reduce search friction.

### Google Search

- Headline 1: `Find Tickets in One Place` (27/30)
- Headline 2: `Search Events by City` (22/30)
- Headline 3: `Find Where to Buy` (17/30)
- Description 1: `Find concerts and sports events, then review seller links in one place.` (73/90)
- Description 2: `Search by event, city, and date. Check availability and fees before checkout.` (78/90)

### Meta

- Primary text: `The ticket hunt is chaotic enough. Find your event, see where to buy, and skip the tab avalanche.` (101/125)
- Headline: `Find Your Next Event` (21/40)
- Description: `Search. Then shop smarter.` (25/30)
