# Paid Ad Creative — 2026-10-22

Three evergreen discovery variants for testing after ad-account access and
conversion measurement are connected. Copy follows the current product-status
guardrails: no price tracking, price history, price drops, alerts, buy/wait
recommendations, savings claims, or TicketScan-generated price data.

## Variation 1 — Find the event

**Angle:** Reduce search friction.

### Google Search

- Headline 1: `Find Tickets in One Place` (27/30)
- Headline 2: `Search Events by City` (22/30)
- Headline 3: `Find Where to Buy` (17/30)
- Description 1: `Find concerts and sports events, then review seller links in one place.` (75/90)
- Description 2: `Search by event, city, and date. Check availability and fees before checkout.` (79/90)

### Meta

- Primary text: `The ticket hunt is chaotic enough. Find your event, see where to buy, and skip the tab avalanche.` (101/125)
- Headline: `Find Your Next Event` (21/40)
- Description: `Search. Then shop smarter.` (25/30)

## Test Setup

- Recommended destination: `/` for broad discovery, or a verified event/city/venue route for intent-specific ad groups.
- Use distinct `utm_content` values: `find-event`, `check-total`, and `venue-guide`.
- Primary test: message match and qualified outbound seller clicks; secondary tests: signup and newsletter completion.
- Do not publish until conversion events are confirmed in the ad platform.
