## Compliance notes

- No price-tracking, price-history, price-drop-alert, target-price, buy/wait,
  savings-statistic, or TicketScan price-data claim is used.
- Avoid “lowest,” “save,” “trending down,” and other unsupported performance
  language in headlines, extensions, and landing pages.
- Confirm the event, onsale date, venue facts, and destination URL before launch.
