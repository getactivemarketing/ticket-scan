## Testing and compliance notes

- Test one variable at a time for at least three attributed days or 100 clicks,
  subject to the approved spend cap.
- Add a unique `utm_content` value to every variation after UTM persistence is
  implemented and verified.
- Do not add price-tracking, price-history, price-drop-alert, target-price,
  buy/wait, lowest-price, urgency, or TicketScan-derived savings language.
- Confirm destination availability and event/venue facts before launch.
