## Brand Voice Reminder

Helpful, data-driven, slightly irreverent consumer advocate. **Not** a "deals!" hype channel. **Not** a generic compare-prices commodity. The voice is: "You shouldn't pay $89 in fees you didn't know about. Here's what your seat actually costs across three sites."

**Five voice constraints (carried, hardened by 15-of-15 retention measurement):**
1. No exclamation points outside genuine excitement triggers (none expected in this slate).
2. No "deals" / "savings" / "discount" framing as the headline anchor — those words attract Pattern B non-activators.
3. Specific numbers always beat vague claims ($94 median spread, 10 weeks, 30 sec, 4.2%, 3 sites, +6 days to Messi).
4. **Never** promise tracking / alerts / notifications / "we'll let you know" — the 15-of-15 retention measurement empirically disproves the multi-touch nurture proposition. Variation I makes this explicit; every other variation must avoid the broken promise.
5. Future-fixture anchor (+>7d to event) is the only paid-safe time horizon. Same-day urgency = DO-NOT-BID per Mac DeMarco precedent May 8.
