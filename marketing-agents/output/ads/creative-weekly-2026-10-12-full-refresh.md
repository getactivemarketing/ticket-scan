and seller-link claims. No price tracking, history, alerts, buy/wait calls,
lowest-price claims, or TicketScan-derived savings statistics are used.

## Display/social image concepts

### D1 — “One event, six checks” checklist

- On-image copy: `Before checkout: event · date · venue · quantity · delivery · final total`
- Supporting copy: `Use a simple checklist before you follow a seller link.`
- Visual direction: clean six-step checklist beside a neutral event-card mockup; no price chart, red discount badge, or urgency countdown.
- Destination: matching event page or `/dashboard`.

### D2 — Venue-first planning

- On-image copy: `Know the venue before you choose a seat`
- Supporting copy: `Review sections, capacity, entry notes, and event details before event day.`
- Visual direction: simplified, fact-checked arena/stadium outline with highlighted sections; use only the named venue's verified guide data.
- Destination: matching verified `/venues/[slug]` page.

## Testing rules

- Change one variable at a time: headline, description, primary text, or image.
- Keep each variant live for at least three clean attributed days or 100 clicks,
  subject to the approved cap.
- Judge downstream actions, not CTR alone.
- Add unique `utm_content` values for every variant.
- Do not upload until conversion events, UTM persistence, and destinations pass QA.
