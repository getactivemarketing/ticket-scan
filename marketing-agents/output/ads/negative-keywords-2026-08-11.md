# Negative Keyword Additions — 2026-08-11

**Status:** Proposed only. No Google Ads search-terms export was available, so these were not applied. Validate against actual query volume before adding broad negatives.

## Candidate exact/phrase negatives

| Theme | Terms |
|---|---|
| Free / giveaway intent | `[free tickets]`, `"free concert tickets"`, `"free sports tickets"`, `giveaway tickets` |
| Seller intent | `[sell my tickets]`, `"sell concert tickets"`, `ticket resale app`, `become a ticket seller` |
| Refund/support intent | `[ticket refund]`, `"cancel ticket order"`, `ticketmaster customer service`, `stubhub support` |
| Scanning / employment | `[ticket scanner]`, `ticket scanning jobs`, `ticket usher jobs`, `ticketmaster jobs` |
| Templates / documents | `[ticket template]`, `"printable ticket template"`, `ticket invoice template` |
| Piracy / non-purchase | `download tickets`, `fake tickets`, `counterfeit tickets` |

## Guardrails

- Do not negative-match `cheap tickets`, `ticket deals`, event names, team names, artist names, venue names, `ticket prices`, or `compare tickets`.
- Keep `ticketscan`, `ticketscan tickets`, and `ticketscan compare` eligible for brand defense.
- Review search terms first; a phrase can carry buying intent in a context the generic term does not reveal.
