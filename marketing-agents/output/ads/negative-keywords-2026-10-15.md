Apply at the narrowest appropriate campaign or ad-group level, preferably as phrase or exact negatives after confirming the query context.

## Do not exclude without query evidence

Do not broadly exclude `cheap tickets`, `last minute tickets`, `discount tickets`, `ticket deals`, event names, team names, artist names, venue names, presale, onsale, or TicketScan brand terms. These can represent legitimate event-discovery or purchase intent.

## Review required

Before import, confirm each query's campaign, ad group, match type, cost, and conversion status. Keep competitor-support negatives scoped to campaigns where customer-service traffic is clearly wasteful.
