# Negative Keyword Additions — 2026-10-16

**Status: Proposed only.** No live Google search-terms report or account write
access was available, so nothing was added. Validate each candidate against the
actual query, campaign, ad group, cost, and conversion status before import.

Apply at the narrowest relevant campaign or ad-group level, preferably as
phrase or exact negatives:

- `ticket refund`
- `refund my tickets`
- `sell my tickets`
- `sell tickets`
- `resell tickets`
- `ticket resale`
- `ticket customer service`
- `ticketmaster customer service`
- `seatgeek customer service`
- `stubhub customer service`
- `ticket login`
- `ticket barcode`
- `printable ticket`
- `ticket template`
- `ticket jobs`

Review `free tickets` at campaign level only; it may be relevant to some event-
discovery campaigns.

Do **not** add `cheap tickets`, `last minute tickets`, `ticket deals`, event,
venue, team, artist, `onsale`, `presale`, `ticket options`, `ticketnetwork`, or
`ticketscan` as account-wide negatives without query evidence. Those can
represent legitimate discovery or buying intent.
