# Candidate Negative Keywords — 2026-10-17

No live Google Ads search-terms report was available, so these are candidates only. They were not applied to an ad account.

## Candidate negatives

| Theme | Exact/phrase candidates | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Free-ticket intent | `free tickets`, `free concert tickets`, `free sports tickets` | Low commercial intent / mismatch |
| Seller workflows | `sell my tickets`, `how to sell tickets`, `ticket resale app` | Seller intent, not discovery |
| Support/refunds | `ticket refund`, `ticketmaster refund`, `ticket cancellation`, `ticket support` | Existing-customer support intent |
| Scanning/venue operations | `ticket scanner`, `ticket scanning app`, `qr ticket scanner` | Product-category mismatch |
| Employment/education | `ticket jobs`, `ticketing jobs`, `ticket pricing tutorial`, `ticket template` | Non-buyer intent |
| Piracy/fraud | `fake tickets`, `print fake tickets`, `ticket hack` | Unsafe / irrelevant intent |

## Keep eligible until query data says otherwise

Do not blanket-negative `ticketmaster`, `seatgeek`, `stubhub`, `tickets`, `concert tickets`, `sports tickets`, or `ticket prices`; these may contain useful discovery or seller-navigation intent. Review the actual query, match type, landing page, and conversion outcome first.
