## Candidate phrase/exact negatives

| Theme | Candidates | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Support/refunds | `ticket refund`, `refund my tickets`, `ticket cancellation`, `ticket support` | Existing-customer support intent |
| Seller workflows | `sell my tickets`, `sell tickets`, `resell tickets`, `ticket resale app` | Seller intent rather than buyer discovery |
| Account/app navigation | `ticket login`, `ticketmaster login`, `seatgeek login`, `stubhub login`, `ticket barcode` | Existing-account or ticket-management intent |
| Non-buyer/DIY | `ticket jobs`, `ticketing jobs`, `ticket template`, `printable ticket` | Employment, education, or format intent |
| Unsafe/irrelevant | `fake tickets`, `print fake tickets`, `ticket hack` | Fraud or irrelevant intent |

Review `free tickets` at campaign level only. Do not blanket-negative `cheap
tickets`, `last minute tickets`, `ticket prices`, event names, team names, artist
names, venue names, `onsale`, `presale`, `ticketmaster`, `ticketnetwork`, or
`ticketscan`; these can represent eligible discovery or sale-window intent.
