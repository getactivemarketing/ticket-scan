**Status: Review only.** No live Google search-terms report or account write
connection was available, so nothing was added. Apply candidates at the
narrowest relevant campaign or ad-group scope after checking the actual query,
match type, cost, and conversion status.

| Theme | Phrase/exact candidates | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Support/refunds | `ticket refund`, `refund my tickets`, `ticket cancellation`, `ticket support` | Existing-customer support intent |
| Seller workflows | `sell my tickets`, `sell tickets`, `resell tickets`, `ticket resale app` | Seller intent rather than buyer discovery |
| Account navigation | `ticket login`, `ticketmaster login`, `seatgeek login`, `stubhub login`, `ticket barcode` | Existing-account or ticket-management intent |
| Non-buyer/DIY | `ticket jobs`, `ticketing jobs`, `ticket template`, `printable ticket` | Employment, education, or format intent |
| Unsafe/irrelevant | `fake tickets`, `print fake tickets`, `ticket hack` | Fraud or irrelevant intent |

Review `free tickets` at campaign level only; it may be valid for some
discovery campaigns. Do not blanket-negative `cheap tickets`, `last minute
tickets`, `ticket deals`, `ticket prices`, event names, team names, artist
names, venue names, `onsale`, `presale`, `ticketmaster`, `ticketnetwork`, or
`ticketscan` without query evidence.
