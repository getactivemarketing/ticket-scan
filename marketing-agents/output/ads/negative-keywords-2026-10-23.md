| Theme | Phrase/exact candidates | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Support/refunds | `ticket refund`, `refund my tickets`, `ticket cancellation`, `ticket support` | Existing-customer support intent |

Review `free tickets` at campaign level only; it can represent valid
discovery intent. Do **not** blanket-negative `cheap tickets`, `last minute
tickets`, `discount tickets`, `ticket deals`, event names, team names, artist
names, venue names, `onsale`, `presale`, `ticketmaster`, `ticketnetwork`, or
`ticketscan` without query evidence.
