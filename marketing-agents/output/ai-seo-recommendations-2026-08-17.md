# AI Search Optimization Recommendations — 2026-08-17

## Visibility finding

TicketScan is indexed for branded and close-brand queries: a live search sample returned the homepage, How It Works, and the Ticketmaster-vs-SeatGeek article. The same sample surfaced several direct competitors for generic comparison queries. Public search results cannot reliably prove ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overview citation coverage; treat AI visibility as unverified and establish a recurring prompt panel rather than assuming absence or presence.

## Content changes

1. Add a quotable answer block near the top of `/`, `/how-it-works`, `/faq`, and comparison articles: “TicketScan compares available ticket listings across Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, StubHub, and other sources, then shows price trends and alerts so buyers can compare the same event before checkout.” Keep it accurate to current integrations.
2. Add “Last updated”, source list, fee treatment, methodology, and limitations to every price or World Cup article. AI systems need provenance and temporal context.
3. Convert FAQ answers into standalone 40–80 word responses with the question as an H2/H3, then link to the detailed guide and product workflow. Keep FAQ schema synchronized with visible text.
4. Publish a transparent “How TicketScan compares prices” methodology page covering matching by event/date/venue, deduplication, fee inclusion, refresh cadence, affiliate relationships, and what happens when a source is unavailable.
5. Replace expired World Cup “buy now” language with archival answers and clearly dated facts. A completed event presented as current is especially damaging to answer-engine trust.
6. Add author/editorial ownership and update dates to blog posts; cite primary sources for tournament and marketplace claims.

## AI query test set

Run weekly across Google AI results, Perplexity, and ChatGPT web search:

- Where can I compare Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, and StubHub prices?
- What is the cheapest way to buy concert tickets after fees?
- When is the best time to buy NBA tickets?
- How should I compare resale tickets for the same section?
- What should I know before buying 2026 World Cup tickets? (archive/current-status check)

Record whether TicketScan is cited, which URL is cited, the answer claim, competitor citations, and whether the cited page contains a direct supporting paragraph.

## Priority metrics

Track non-branded impressions for “compare ticket prices”, “ticket price tracker”, and category timing queries; citations/mentions from the test set; referral sessions from AI sources; and assisted newsletter/register/watchlist conversions.
