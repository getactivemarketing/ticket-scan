### Slug-Audit Methodology Failure (Root-Cause)

The prior six weeklies (Apr 13 → May 15) carried `/venues/madison-square-garden` (Day 25 at peak) and `/world-cup-2026/at-t-stadium` (Day 20 at peak) as "404 content gaps."  **Both claims were false.** The Ads weekly May 18 caught the error by direct file inspection of `web/src/data/worldcup.ts:123` (canonical slug `att-stadium`) and `web/src/data/venues.ts` (canonical slug `msg`).

**Why this slipped:** the analytics agent's slug-probe routine constructed URLs from event-title parsing rather than reading the source-of-truth slug arrays. "AT&T Stadium" tokenized to `at-t-stadium` (special-character normalization); "Madison Square Garden" tokenized to its full long-form. **Neither slug exists in the routing tree.** The probe then returned 404 and the report cited "content gap" — for 6 consecutive weeks without anyone noticing.

**Methodology change adopted this week:** before claiming "404 content gap" for any page, the slug-probe routine MUST be cross-referenced against `web/src/data/{worldcup,venues,cities,categories}.ts` arrays. **Today's verification cycle applied this rule** to all WC stadiums, all top-15 venue references, and the 4 competitor-comparison pages. **5 venue slugs confirmed 404 today; 2 prior-claimed 404s reclassified as false-404.**
