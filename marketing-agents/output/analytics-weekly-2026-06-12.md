## Competitor Deep-Dive (rotation: StubHub)

> Sourced from general product knowledge (assistant cutoff Jan 2026), **not** live recon —
> this agent has no live competitive-intelligence tooling. Treat as directional, not current.

**StubHub — positioning.** Resale-marketplace-first (FanProtect guarantee, all-in pricing
toggle rolled out under regulatory pressure). Strength is inventory breadth and brand
recognition for secondary tickets; weakness for us to exploit is that StubHub is a
_marketplace_, not a _price-comparison_ tool — it shows its own listings, not a
cross-platform "is this the best price anywhere" view. That cross-source comparison is
exactly TicketScan's intended wedge — **which is undercut while `/compare` is login-gated.**

**Opportunity (honest version):** the differentiator only matters if a logged-out visitor
can actually _see_ a comparison. Un-gating `/compare` is therefore both a CRO fix and the
thing that makes the StubHub-vs-us positioning real.

**Comparison-page spec:** Per standing memory, four `/compare/ticketscan-vs-*` competitor
pages have been spec'd repeatedly and none have shipped (all 404). **I am not adding a new
spec.** Recommendation to Content/Eng: ship **one** page end-to-end (`/compare/ticketscan-vs-stubhub`)
before spec'ing any more. One shipped page beats four specs.
