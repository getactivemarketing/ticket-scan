## Competitor Deep-Dive (rotation: Vivid Seats)

> Directional, from general product knowledge (cutoff Jan 2026) — no live recon tooling.

**Positioning.** Vivid Seats is a secondary-marketplace + loyalty play ("Vivid Seats
Rewards" — buy 10 get 1 free credit), heavy on TV/affiliate and sports-team partnerships.
Like StubHub it sells *its own* inventory; it is not a cross-platform price comparison.

**Gap we can exploit:** Vivid's rewards program locks value behind repeat purchases on one
marketplace. TicketScan's wedge is the opposite — *"is this the best price anywhere, right
now"* across sources, no loyalty lock-in. **But that wedge is invisible while `/compare` is
login-gated.** Same conclusion as the StubHub week: the differentiator is real only if a
logged-out visitor can see the comparison.

**Comparison-page spec:** Per standing memory, four `/compare/ticketscan-vs-*` pages have
been spec'd and none shipped (all 404). **Not adding a fifth spec.** Recommendation: ship
**one** page end-to-end before spec'ing more.
