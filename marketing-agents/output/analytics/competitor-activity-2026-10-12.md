## Implication for TicketScan

The useful consumer-facing response is better event discovery context: accurate onsale/presale information, venue-specific guidance, seller-link clarity, and plain-English fee checks. Do not counter SeatGeek’s pricing narrative with TicketScan price claims while TicketScan’s own price data remains stale.
