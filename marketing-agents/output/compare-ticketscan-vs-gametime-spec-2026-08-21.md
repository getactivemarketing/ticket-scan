# Comparison Page Spec: TicketScan vs. Gametime

**Audience:** Content Agent 1  
**Suggested URL:** `/compare/ticketscan-vs-gametime`

## Search intent and promise

Answer: “Should I use Gametime or TicketScan before buying?” Position Gametime as the transaction marketplace for curated, often last-minute inventory; position TicketScan as the independent pre-purchase comparison and timing layer.

## Page outline

1. H1: TicketScan vs. Gametime: Compare Before You Buy
2. Short answer with a neutral “use both” recommendation.
3. Feature matrix: marketplace inventory, checkout, all-in pricing, mobile delivery, buyer protection, cross-market comparison, price history, target-price alerts, buy/wait guidance.
4. Explain Gametime’s all-in pricing, curated deals, last-minute focus, and Ticket Coverage with official citations.
5. Explain TicketScan’s sources, timestamps, normalization, history, and recommendation logic—only claim features that are live and fresh.
6. “Which is better for you?” decision cards: last-minute purchase / compare several sources / track a target price / buyer protection.
7. Methodology and disclosure: prices vary, fees and inventory change, TicketScan is not the seller.
8. CTA: “Search your event” and “Compare ticket prices.”

## SEO

- Primary: `TicketScan vs Gametime`
- Secondary: `Gametime alternative`, `Gametime vs SeatGeek`, `last minute ticket price comparison`, `ticket price tracker`
- Internal links: `/compare`, `/how-it-works`, `/faq`, relevant city/venue guides.

## Acceptance criteria

- No unsupported savings, traffic, ranking, or real-time claims.
- Every competitor feature claim links to an official Gametime source.
- Include “last checked” timestamps for any example price.
- Add FAQ schema only for visible FAQs.
- Validate anonymous access; `/compare` should not require signup before a useful preview.
