# Comparison Page Spec: TicketScan vs SeatGeek

**Path:** `/compare/ticketscan-vs-seatgeek`

**Title:** TicketScan vs. SeatGeek: Event Research, Venue Guides, and Ticket Buying

**Meta description:** Compare TicketScan’s event research and venue guides with SeatGeek’s ticket marketplace, discovery tools, and checkout experience.

## Primary Angle

TicketScan helps shoppers research events, venues, and onsale timing. SeatGeek is a ticket marketplace where shoppers can browse inventory and complete a purchase.

## Required Sections

1. What each service is.
2. Factual feature matrix: event discovery, venue guides, seating context, onsale/presale information, saved events, conversational search, live inventory, seat maps, checkout, delivery, seller support, and buyer protections.
3. Decision path: researching venue/date/options → TicketScan; ready to browse inventory and buy in one marketplace → SeatGeek.
4. Fee and protection checklist: compare the final all-in total and read current restrictions. Do not assert a fee advantage without a controlled current checkout test.
5. Editorial disclosure: TicketScan is not the seller; outbound links may be affiliate links.

## Suggested Matrix

| Capability | TicketScan | SeatGeek |
|---|---|---|
| Event discovery | Yes, with outbound seller links | Yes, with marketplace inventory |
| Venue sections/capacity/tips | Yes, venue-guide focus | Seat maps and seat views on supported events |
| Onsale/presale timing | Yes, where verified | Event-dependent |
| Live marketplace checkout | No | Yes |
| Conversational search | Not currently | Ask SeatGeek |
| Saved events | Yes | Yes / event notifications vary by product flow |
| Buyer guarantee and fulfillment | Not applicable; TicketScan is not the seller | SeatGeek publishes marketplace guarantee and delivery terms |
| Independent research context | Core position | Secondary to transaction/discovery |

## Prohibited Copy

Do not say TicketScan currently tracks price history, sends price-drop alerts, issues buy/wait/hold calls, compares live TicketScan prices, or guarantees savings. Do not imply TicketScan provides SeatGeek’s buyer protections or checkout.

## CTA

Primary: “Find an event and check the venue.” Secondary: “Browse tickets on SeatGeek.” Keep the seller disclosure adjacent to outbound links.
