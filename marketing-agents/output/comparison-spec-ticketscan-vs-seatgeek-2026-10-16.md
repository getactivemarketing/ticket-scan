# Comparison Page Spec: TicketScan vs. SeatGeek

**Owner:** Content Agent
**Target path:** `/compare/ticketscan-vs-seatgeek`
**Date:** 2026-10-16

## Search Intent

Serve readers deciding whether they need an independent event/venue research workflow or a marketplace where they can browse inventory and complete checkout.

## SEO Fields

- **Title:** TicketScan vs. SeatGeek: Event Research, Venue Guides, and Ticket Buying
- **Meta description:** Compare TicketScan’s event research and venue guides with SeatGeek’s ticket marketplace, discovery tools, and checkout experience.

## Positioning

TicketScan helps shoppers research events, understand venue and onsale details, save events, and follow seller links. SeatGeek is a ticket marketplace with consumer discovery, conversational search, live inventory, and checkout.

## Required Sections

1. What each service is.
2. Factual feature matrix:
   - event discovery;
   - venue guides and seating context;
   - onsale/presale information;
   - saved events;
   - conversational search;
   - live inventory;
   - seat maps;
   - checkout and delivery;
   - seller support and buyer protections.
3. Decision guide:
   - researching an event, date, venue, and seating context → TicketScan;
   - ready to browse inventory and buy in a marketplace → SeatGeek.
4. Checkout checklist: compare the final all-in total, verify date/venue/section, confirm delivery timing, and read current seller restrictions.
5. Editorial disclosure: TicketScan is not the ticket seller or transaction guarantor; outbound links may be affiliate links.

## Copy Guardrails

- Do not claim TicketScan currently tracks prices, records price history, sends price-drop or target-price alerts, issues buy/wait/hold calls, compares live TicketScan prices, or guarantees savings.
- Do not claim SeatGeek has a specific fee advantage without a controlled, current checkout test.
- Describe SeatGeek’s TourIQ as a SeatGeekIQ promoter/venue product, not as a consumer feature unless the page explicitly says so.
- Keep all TicketScan feature claims tied to the current site: event discovery, venue facts, onsale/presale timing, watchlist/save behavior, and seller links.

## Suggested Table

| Capability | TicketScan | SeatGeek |
|---|---|---|
| Find events | Yes, with outbound seller links | Yes, marketplace discovery |
| Venue seating/capacity guides | Yes, editorial guides | Varies by event/venue |
| Onsale/presale timing | Yes, where sourced | Event-dependent |
| Save events | Yes, watchlist | Verify current consumer flow before publication |
| Conversational search | Not currently documented | Ask SeatGeek |
| Live ticket inventory and checkout | No; links out | Yes |
| Mobile marketplace delivery | No | Yes, subject to listing/event terms |
| Transaction protection | Not provided by TicketScan | Describe only current SeatGeek terms and link to them |
