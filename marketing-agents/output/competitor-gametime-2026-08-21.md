# Competitor Analysis: Gametime — 2026-08-21

## Product

Gametime is a mobile-first last-minute ticket marketplace. Its public materials emphasize all-in pricing, curated deal tiers, venue maps, mobile delivery, price adjustments near event time, LastCall purchases after an event starts, and buyer protection. Gametime’s 2026 materials also describe included Ticket Coverage and a lower-price match credit equal to 110% of the difference. Sources: [Gametime pricing](https://gametime.co/blog/the-ins-and-outs-of-pricing-on-gametime/), [Ticket Coverage](https://gametime.co/blog/announcing-gametime-ticket-coverage/), [market coverage](https://gametime.co/blog/about/).

- **Gametime has that TicketScan does not:** checkout/inventory, mobile delivery, curated seat tiers, map/seat context, buyer protection, and a strong last-minute use case.
- **TicketScan can have that Gametime does not:** source-neutral comparison across marketplaces, historical cross-market context, and a buy/wait recommendation.
- **UX difference:** Gametime compresses the path to a purchase; TicketScan should compress the path to a confident decision.
- **Pricing model:** marketplace ticket price plus disclosed fees/all-in presentation; exact fee treatment should be checked at checkout.

## Marketing

- **SEO/content:** Gametime publishes event- and category-specific buying guides, pricing explainers, legitimacy/safety content, and proprietary market stories. It also publishes competitor comparison pages such as Gametime vs. SeatGeek and Gametime vs. StubHub.
- **Positioning:** fast, curated, fan-friendly access to good last-minute deals.
- **Social/paid/email:** public channel performance, spend, and email metrics were not available; do not infer engagement from publishing volume.
- **Editorial advantage:** it turns marketplace data into timely stories, including sports and World Cup pricing coverage.

## Opportunities

1. Own “should I buy now?” with timestamped comparisons across Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, StubHub, and Gametime where available.
2. Explain the difference between Gametime’s marketplace price guarantee and an independent market-wide check.
3. Target “Gametime alternative,” “Gametime vs SeatGeek,” “last-minute ticket price tracker,” and “are ticket prices dropping?”
4. Build a last-minute decision page: event urgency, current source spread, price-history freshness, and a clear data timestamp.
5. Add a neutral “use both” workflow: TicketScan for context, Gametime for protected checkout.

## Threats

- Gametime’s all-in pricing reduces fee anxiety before checkout.
- LastCall and mobile delivery create urgency and convenience TicketScan cannot match.
- Curated deal tiers and proprietary editorial data can make an external comparison feel unnecessary.
- Its existing competitor-comparison content can capture the same commercial-intent keywords.

## Strategic Recommendation

Do not imitate the marketplace. Repair price freshness and alerts, then position TicketScan as the independent pre-purchase check: “See whether today’s listing is actually a good deal before you pay.”
