# Competitor Analysis: Vivid Seats — 2026-08-14

## Product

Vivid Seats is a transaction marketplace with event search, filters, seat maps, price sorting, checkout, and customer support. Its public materials emphasize all-in pricing, a 100% Buyer Guarantee, Rewards, and app-based loyalty. Its published terms describe a Lowest Price Guarantee that compares eligible resale listings from SeatGeek, StubHub, and Ticketmaster Verified Resale for comparable event/date/venue/section/row/quantity criteria. Sources: [Vivid buying guide](https://www.vividseats.com/blog/company/how-to-buy-tickets-from-vivid-seats), [Buyer Guarantee](https://www.vividseats.com/guarantee/), [terms and Lowest Price Guarantee](https://www.vividseats.com/en/terms), [Rewards](https://www.vividseats.com/rewards?irgwc=1).

**Vivid is stronger at:** inventory, checkout, delivery/support, buyer protection, loyalty, app distribution, and a purchase-linked price guarantee.

**TicketScan can be stronger at:** source-neutral discovery across Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, StubHub, and future sources; historical market context; and a buy/wait recommendation before the user commits to a marketplace. This is a strategic advantage only after the price tracker is current.

## Marketing

- **SEO/content:** Vivid publishes buying and safety education, venue/event merchandising, and loyalty-oriented content. Its content connects reassurance directly to marketplace checkout and guarantee language.
- **Positioning:** trusted, rewarding ticket marketplace: all-in cost visibility, protected transactions, and repeat-buyer rewards.
- **Email/social/paid:** no public campaign-level performance data was available. Do not infer engagement from publishing volume.
- **Offer strategy:** Rewards turns repeat purchases into a retention loop; the Lowest Price Guarantee is a powerful comparison objection handler.

## Positioning

Vivid targets fans ready to purchase and willing to value protection, inventory, and rewards. TicketScan should target the earlier decision moment: fans asking whether a listing is fairly priced, which source is cheapest for equivalent tickets, and whether to buy now or wait.

## Opportunities

1. Build “Vivid Seats vs. the market” comparisons around same event, equivalent section/quantity, all-in total, source, timestamp, and delivery terms.
2. Explain the difference between a marketplace’s lowest-price guarantee and an independent market-wide comparison.
3. Create an all-in-fees glossary and a “before you buy” checklist that links directly into `/compare`.
4. Add a neutral “use both” workflow: TicketScan for context, Vivid (or another source) for protected checkout.
5. Once data is healthy, publish anonymized price-history examples rather than unsupported savings claims.

## Threats

- Vivid’s all-in pricing and guarantee reduce the perceived need for an external checker.
- Rewards and app loyalty create repeat behavior TicketScan does not yet match.
- Its Lowest Price Guarantee directly answers the comparison objection.
- Vivid controls the final checkout and customer-support relationship; TicketScan does not.

## Strategic Recommendation

Own the pre-purchase intelligence layer, not marketplace fulfillment. The wedge is: “Check the market before you buy.” Product proof requires fresh price history, working alerts, comparable-listing normalization, and explicit timestamps.
