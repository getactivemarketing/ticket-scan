# Content Agent 1 Handoff — SEO Week of 2026-08-10

## Priority briefs

### 1. Ticket price comparison methodology

- **Target:** `/how-it-works` expansion or a new `/methodology` page.
- **Intent:** how ticket comparison works; are ticket prices comparable; how fees and price history are handled.
- **Answer to lead with:** TicketScan compares like-for-like listings across supported sellers, labels source/timestamp/fee basis, and sends users to the seller for checkout.
- **Required sections:** source coverage, matching and deduplication, price history, all-in vs displayed prices, missing data, buy/wait/hold logic, limitations, FAQ, alert CTA.
- **Evidence:** use only behavior implemented in the product; include a dated example with event, section, source, and price basis.

### 2. Best time to buy concert tickets

- **Target:** `/best-time-to-buy/concerts` pilot.
- **Intent:** best time to buy concert tickets; should I wait for prices to drop.
- **Required sections:** direct answer, days-to-event chart, sample size/date range, exceptions, primary vs resale, fees, alert CTA, FAQ.
- **Guardrail:** no universal “Tuesday is cheapest” claim without TicketScan data.

### 3. World Cup 2026 ticket price archive

- **Target:** refresh hub and stadium copy; preserve URLs.
- **Intent:** what World Cup tickets cost, stadium price history, final/semi-final ticket archive.
- **Required sections:** completed-event notice, observed prices with dates/currency/source, results, venue facts, methodology, current-events CTA.
- **Remove:** active buying promises, “act fast,” and current inventory language.

### 4. Platform comparison cluster

- **Targets:** `/compare-guides/ticketmaster-vs-seatgeek`, `/compare-guides/seatgeek-vs-stubhub`, `/compare-guides/ticketmaster-vs-stubhub`.
- **Intent:** which marketplace is cheapest, fees, protection, primary vs resale.
- **Required sections:** dated verdict, like-for-like table, fees/all-in caveat, buyer protection, inventory differences, when TicketScan helps, FAQ.

## Editorial standards

Every brief needs an author/reviewer, visible updated date, cited first-party or reproducible evidence, one direct answer paragraph, and links to a relevant hub plus product action.
