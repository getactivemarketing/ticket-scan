# Content briefs for Content Agent — SEO week of 2026-10-26

These are internal briefs. Do not claim price history, price trends, alerts,
buy/wait/hold calls, or TicketScan price statistics.

## Brief 1: Onsale vs presale vs general sale

- **Working title:** Onsale vs Presale vs General Sale: What Ticket Dates Mean
- **Search intent:** informational; users trying to understand a ticket date.
- **Answer first:** define each term in three sentences and explain that the
  event page/seller is the authority for the exact time and eligibility.
- **Outline:** definitions; time zones; presale-code sources; public onsale;
  waitlist/queue expectations; official seller verification; checklist.
- **Internal links:** `/onsales`, `/how-it-works`, one city page, one venue page.
- **Avoid:** “we’ll alert you,” “buy now,” “best time,” or any price claim.

## Brief 2: How to read a venue seating chart

- **Working title:** How to Read a Stadium or Arena Seating Chart
- **Search intent:** practical venue planning.
- **Outline:** bowl levels; sideline/endline/corner; suites and clubs; why
  concert layouts change; accessibility/entry checks; use the event-specific
  map; three verified TicketScan venue examples.
- **Internal links:** three venue guides, the relevant city hub, venue guide hub.
- **Evidence:** use only `venue.sections`, capacity, and official venue facts.
- **Avoid:** “cheapest section,” “best value,” or resale price claims; use
  sightline/access language instead.

## Brief 3: Ticket fees checklist

- **Working title:** Ticket Fees Checklist: Compare the Total Before Checkout
- **Search intent:** buyer education.
- **Outline:** listing vs all-in total; service/delivery/order fees; taxes;
  mobile delivery; refund/resale rules; screenshot/record the terms before
  payment; use official seller checkout.
- **Internal links:** `/how-it-works`, `/faq`, one event search CTA.
- **Avoid:** claiming TicketScan currently compares prices or fees live.

## Brief 4: World Cup host-stadium guide after the tournament

- **Working title:** 2026 World Cup Host Stadiums: Seating, Access, and Match
  Memories
- **Search intent:** evergreen/historical reference after the tournament.
- **Outline:** 16 host venues; city/capacity; seating configuration; access;
  match schedule/results labeled historical; links to stadium pages.
- **Internal links:** `/world-cup-2026` and all relevant stadium pages.
- **Avoid:** current resale prices, historical price statistics presented as
  current, or TicketScan alert/comparison claims.

## Editorial QA gate

Before handoff, verify every factual statement has a data/source reference,
every date is labeled current or historical, and forbidden product-status terms
are absent from headline, excerpt, body, CTA, metadata, and image alt text.
