## Competitor content gaps to exploit

- **Cross-market comparison:** SeatGeek and StubHub tend to own their own inventory pages. TicketScan can own “same event, three marketplaces, all-in total” pages.
- **Price history and timing:** Competitor guides explain how to buy, but rarely connect timing advice to a tracked price history and a user-set target.
- **Post-event learning:** The World Cup creates a timely bridge page about official vs resale pricing, fees, transfer risk, and what to repeat for fall sports.
- **City + event combinations:** Build indexable event pages with date, venue, lowest available price when present, seating context, and FAQs. Keep values null-safe when inventory is absent.
- **Conversion specificity:** Pair every informational page with one action: compare, add to watchlist, set a target, or download the checklist.
