## Content-gap response vs competitors

- **Neutral all-in-price methodology:** explain what TicketScan can compare, what fees may be disclosed later, and what the buyer must verify at checkout.
- **Actionable timing by event type:** competitors offer general advice; TicketScan can pair timing guidance with a watchlist and target-price workflow.
- **Same event / same seat comparison format:** show platform, section, listing price, fees when available, total when available, timestamp, and source.
- **Session-level sports guides:** the US Open creates a useful gap around day vs evening sessions, early rounds vs championship week, and grounds/outer-court access.
- **Activation content:** a concise “find event → compare → watch → set target” walkthrough is more product-specific than generic ticket-buying advice.

## Email handoff — Agent 5

Deliver the checklist immediately after signup. Suggested sequence:

- Day 0: “Your overpay checklist is ready” + one-minute first use.
- Day 2: “The cheapest listing is not always the cheapest ticket” + all-in-price explanation.
- Day 5: “Turn a warning sign into a watchlist” + setup CTA.
- Day 10: “Set a ceiling before the event gets urgent” + target-price CTA.
- Day 21: “Your next ticket check” + comparison CTA.

Do not promise a price drop or savings amount. Suppress users who already have a target price set from the activation reminder.
