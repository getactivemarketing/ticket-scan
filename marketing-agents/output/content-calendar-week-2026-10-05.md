## Why these topics now

- The NFL official Week 5 schedule includes October 8 Thursday Night Football and October 11 Sunday games, creating immediate game-specific search intent.
- The NHL regular season started September 29; October 6 includes Islanders–Rangers, and the league’s October schedule is active.

## Refresh rules

1. Use official venue, team, league, artist, promoter, or ticketing help pages for changed facts.
2. Preserve existing verified capacity and section data unless a new official source supports a change.
3. Remove broad “lowest prices,” “price history,” and “we track prices” language from any page touched.
4. Every event page should distinguish the event date, venue, sale type, and seller; conditional postseason games need an explicit conditional label.
5. Do not edit `web/src` from this content-planning run. Send fact-checked proposals to `marketing-agents/output/content/refresh-proposals/` for review.
