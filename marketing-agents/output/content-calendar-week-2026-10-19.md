## Competitor gap and editorial edge

SeatGeek and StubHub cover marketplace browsing, fee explainers, and presale mechanics. TicketScan’s defensible editorial angle is a neutral event-research layer:

- verify the event, date, venue, and local onsale time;
- explain what the section and sightline labels mean at that building;
- distinguish standard tickets, resale tickets, VIP packages, parking, and waitlists;
- tell readers to expand the all-in checkout total and read delivery terms;
- link readers to event discovery and the available seller destinations without promising a current price comparison.
