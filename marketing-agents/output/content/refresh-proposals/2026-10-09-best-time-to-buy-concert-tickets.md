> ## When should you buy concert tickets?
>
> There is no universal magic day that guarantees the cheapest concert ticket. The useful question is whether you are still choosing among dates and sections, or whether the event is close enough that availability matters more than patience. Start with the official onsale and presale dates, then compare the same event and seat area across available sellers.
>
> ### Start with the onsale calendar
>
> Presales can have separate registration rules, windows, and inventory from the public onsale. Ticketmaster explains that presales may require an event-specific code and do not guarantee tickets. [Ticketmaster presale guidance](https://help.ticketmaster.com/hc/en-us/articles/9702132309905-How-do-presales-work) Save the event date and the onsale time in your calendar, and use the venue or artist’s official page as the source of truth when details conflict.
>
> ### Compare the all-in total
>
> A low headline number is not a finished comparison. Check the total after mandatory fees before deciding which listing is actually cheaper. The Federal Trade Commission’s live-event fee rule requires covered sellers to show the total price, inclusive of mandatory fees, before the consumer pays; its FAQ explains the rule and its scope. [FTC source](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/rule-unfair-or-deceptive-fees-frequently-asked-questions)

Replace the “Price Patterns by Genre” table with:

> ### What to compare before you buy
>
> - **Date:** A Tuesday matinee and a Saturday night show are different shopping decisions; check the exact performance.
> - **Seat location:** Compare like with like. A floor listing, lower bowl listing, and upper bowl listing are not interchangeable just because they are for the same artist.
> - **Delivery:** Confirm whether the ticket is mobile, transferable, or subject to an event-specific delivery window.
> - **Total cost:** Review mandatory fees, taxes, and delivery charges at checkout.
> - **Seller:** Use the official event page and established marketplaces, and read the listing terms before paying.
