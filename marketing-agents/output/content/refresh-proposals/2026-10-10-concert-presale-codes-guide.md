# Refresh proposal: Concert Presale Codes Guide

**Page:** `/blog/concert-presale-codes-guide`

**File to change:** `web/src/data/blog.ts`, blog post with `slug: 'concert-presale-codes-guide'`.

## Why this page needs a refresh

The post is useful, but several lines are stale or overpromise: it describes presales as the best route to face value, gives a fixed Monday-to-Friday timeline, says resale prices are often near face value, and ends with a price-alert CTA. The refresh should keep the practical presale checklist while making the official event page and seller terms the source of truth.

## Exact replacement text

Replace the opening with:

> Presales can give eligible fans an earlier chance to shop, but access, inventory, timing, and codes vary by event. Start with the artist, venue, promoter, and official ticket page; then read the specific presale instructions before you join the queue.

Replace the “Presale Timeline” section with:

> ### The timeline is event-specific
>
> There is no reliable Monday-to-Friday script for every tour. An announcement may be followed by artist registration, a fan-club or sponsor window, a venue or promoter window, and a general onsale — or some of those steps may not exist at all. Ticketmaster says artist presale signup can send a reminder before the sale, but signing up does not guarantee tickets. Check the event page for the exact date, time zone, eligibility rules, ticket limit, and whether a code is required.
>
> Put the onsale time in your calendar using the venue or artist's published time zone. If two pages disagree, use the official event page or contact the venue before clicking a third-party link.

Replace the “When Presales Sell Out” section with:

> ### If you miss the presale
>
> Check the event's general onsale and any additional official presales listed on the event page. Inventory can differ between presales, so a sold-out presale does not by itself prove that the event is sold out.
>
> If you consider a resale listing, compare the exact date, section, row, delivery method, seller, refund terms, and final all-in total. Ticketmaster's delivery guidance notes that some events are mobile-entry only and that delivery timing can vary, so do not assume every listing is immediately transferable.

Replace the closing CTA with:

> Use TicketScan to find upcoming events and onsale information, then follow the linked seller's checkout terms. Before paying, confirm the exact performance, seat location, delivery method, and final total — because a mystery fee at the last click is still a fee.
