## Page CRO Review — `/compare` (web/src/app/compare/page.tsx)

### Critical friction (P0)
1. **Hard auth wall before any value** (lines 52–56). Unauthenticated users are bounced to `/login` instantly — they never see the comparison UI, savings preview, or even know what they're signing up for. This is the single biggest leak on the page.
   - **Fix:** Allow anonymous search. Show 1–2 matched results with full pricing, then gate the *3rd result* (or "Save This Search / Set Price Alert") behind signup. Pattern: Kayak/Skyscanner.
   - **Expected lift:** 3–6× signup rate from `/compare` traffic (most CRO case studies on auth walls).

2. **Empty state buries the value prop** (lines 530–545). The hero says "Compare Ticket Prices" but doesn't quantify savings. Replace with a concrete claim:
   - "Users save an average of $[X] per ticket vs buying on the first site they check."
   - Add 2–3 logo strip (Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, StubHub, Vivid) for trust — already listed but as plain emoji text.

3. **Default city is hardcoded "Orlando"** (line 40). Geo-IP detect or remember last search. Orlando users convert; everyone else has to retype.

### Medium (P1)
4. **CTA copy is fine** ("Compare Prices") — keep. ✅
5. **No social proof on results.** Add "X users tracking this event" or "Lowest price in 30 days" badge on matched cards (you already have `price_history` data — wire it in).
6. **"Save $X" badge** (line 343) only renders when `bestSource && savings > 0`. Good — but make it bigger and add a secondary "Set alert when this drops below $Y" CTA → drives watchlist adds (the real conversion goal).
7. **Mobile:** 4-column form grid collapses to 1 col on `sm` (line 195). Test that the date pickers don't trigger horizontal scroll on iPhone SE width (375px).

### Low (P2)
8. Pro Tip banner (548) is below the fold and reads as defensive ("fees may apply"). Move the *positive* part ("60+ resale sites") into the empty state, drop the disclaimer or shrink it.
9. Emoji-heavy headers (⚖️ 🎫 🎟️) feel playful but inconsistent with a "data-driven" brand voice. Pick one direction.

### Recommended A/B test #1
- **Hypothesis:** Removing the auth wall on `/compare` and gating only watchlist/alert actions will lift signup conversions ≥2×.
- **Variant A (control):** Current — redirect to login.
- **Variant B:** Anonymous search allowed; "Set Price Alert" button on each card triggers signup modal.
- **Primary metric:** Signups attributed to `/compare` sessions.
- **Sample size:** ~2 weeks at current traffic (revisit once analytics are live).
