## Page CRO Review — World Cup Pages (`/world-cup-2026` + `/world-cup-2026/[stadium]`)

World Cup pages are the highest-intent pages on the site for 2026. With the tournament 2 months away (June 11), these pages should be converting at maximum efficiency. Instead, they are the weakest-converting pages relative to their traffic potential — functioning as brochure pages rather than conversion-optimized landing pages.

### Critical (P0)

1. **Zero email capture on World Cup pages.**
   The `<NewsletterSignup>` component exists, supports a `source` prop, and is used on the homepage in two placements. Neither the main World Cup landing page nor any of the 16 stadium pages use it. Users browsing World Cup content who aren't ready to create an account have no low-commitment way to stay connected.
   - **Fix:** Add `<NewsletterSignup source="world-cup-2026" variant="card" />` to `/world-cup-2026/page.tsx` between the Ticket Tips section and the bottom CTA. Customize heading: "Get World Cup Ticket Alerts — be first to know when prices drop."
   - **Fix (stadium pages):** Add `<NewsletterSignup source="world-cup-[slug]" variant="inline" />` inside a `lg:hidden` block at the top of the page body on stadium pages, so mobile users see a capture form before scrolling through all the content.
   - **Expected lift:** 5–10% email capture rate (industry benchmark for contextual signup on content pages).

2. **Stadium pages: CTAs buried on mobile.**
   The layout uses `lg:grid-cols-3` — on mobile, the right-column CTA widgets ("Search Tickets" and "Create Free Account") fall below the About section, Seating & Pricing section, and Ticket Tips. A mobile user must scroll through 800+ words of content before seeing any conversion element.
   - **Fix:** Add a mobile-first CTA block (`lg:hidden`) immediately below the hero on stadium pages:
     ```
     [Search World Cup Tickets at {venue}] [Set a Price Alert — Free]
     ```
   - **Impact:** Moves the primary conversion action from below-fold to above-fold on mobile. Expected 30–50% increase in CTA visibility on mobile.

3. **No social proof anywhere on World Cup pages.**
   The homepage shows "50K+ Events Tracked", "$127 Avg. Savings", "5+ Sites Compared" plus 3 testimonials. World Cup pages — where first-time organic visitors land via searches like "world cup 2026 tickets cheap" — show zero platform credibility signals.
   - **Fix:** Port the homepage stats bar to the World Cup landing page, directly below the hero stats grid. Add one sports-specific testimonial.
   - **Fix (stadium pages):** Add a compact trust line below the hero: "Comparing prices across Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, and StubHub."

4. **Bottom CTA on stadium pages is a navigation link, not a conversion CTA.**
   "View All World Cup Venues" sends users sideways to browse more venues instead of converting. This is the last thing a user sees after reading venue-specific content — peak intent, wasted on a directory link.
   - **Fix:** Replace with: "Set a Price Alert for [venue name] — get notified when World Cup tickets drop." Link to `/register?source=world-cup-[slug]`. Keep the "View All Venues" as a secondary text link beneath it.

### High (P1)

5. **Hero headline is descriptive, not benefit-led.**
   "2026 FIFA World Cup Tickets" is SEO-correct but doesn't drive action. Compare with the homepage's "Compare. Track. Save." which immediately communicates value.
   - **Fix:** Test "Never Overpay for World Cup 2026 Tickets" or "Find the Cheapest World Cup 2026 Tickets" as headline, keeping "2026 FIFA World Cup Tickets" as the H1 for SEO in the metadata.
   - **Proposed A/B Test #4:** Current headline vs "Never Overpay for World Cup 2026 Tickets" — primary metric: hero CTA click-through rate.

6. **"Find Tickets" buttons on match rows have undersized touch targets.**
   Match row CTA buttons use `px-4 py-2` — approximately 36px tall. Apple's HIG recommends 44pt minimum.
   - **Fix:** Increase to `py-3` for adequate touch targets. Also applies to the date picker pills in the match schedule.

7. **World Cup nav indicator is not animated.**
   The green dot next to "World Cup 2026" in the navbar is a static `bg-green-400 rounded-full` div. The visual intent is urgency/newness, but it doesn't pulse.
   - **Fix:** Add `animate-pulse` class: `className="w-2 h-2 bg-green-400 rounded-full inline-block animate-pulse"`.

8. **Desktop nav CTA says "Sign Up" while mobile says "Sign Up Free".**
   "Free" is a conversion-positive word. The inconsistency suggests mobile was optimized separately.
   - **Fix:** Change desktop CTA to "Sign Up Free" to match mobile.

### Medium (P2)

9. **Match schedule date picker has invisible horizontal scroll on mobile.**
   Uses `overflow-x-auto scrollbar-hide` — many mobile users won't discover there are more dates to the right.
   - **Fix:** Add a fade gradient on the right edge and/or a "scroll for more dates" hint on first load.

10. **Hero CTAs have inconsistent keyword intent.**
    Hero primary: `/dashboard?keyword=world+cup+2026`. Bottom secondary: `/dashboard?keyword=world+cup` (missing "2026"). Match row buttons: `/dashboard?keyword=world+cup+{city}` — but cities like "East Rutherford" are not natural search terms.
    - **Fix:** Standardize all search links to use `world+cup+2026` and add the venue name where applicable.

11. **Stadium page pricing uses confidence-undermining language.**
    "Estimated range" and "Prices are estimates based on similar events" are honest but reduce urgency. Users see $800–$2500 as an unhelpful range rather than a trigger to track.
    - **Fix:** Rephrase to "Resale prices from $800 — set an alert to get the best deal" to maintain honesty while building urgency.

12. **No "share" or social distribution on World Cup pages.**
    World Cup content is inherently shareable — friends plan trips together. Adding share buttons creates viral distribution at zero cost.

## Form CRO — Price Alert Setup

### Current State
**There is no dedicated price alert form.** Setting a price alert requires:
1. Create an account (3-field registration form)
2. Search for the event
3. Click "Add to Watchlist"
4. Set a target price in the watchlist modal

This is a 4-step process with a registration wall for what should be a single-action conversion. For World Cup pages — where users have specific price sensitivity ("I'll go if tickets are under $500") — the friction is conversion-killing.

### Recommendation: Build an Inline Price Alert Micro-Form

**Placement:** World Cup landing page (above bottom CTA) + stadium pages (right sidebar)

**Fields:**
| Field | Type | Required | Notes |
|-------|------|----------|-------|
| Email | email | Yes | Pre-fills registration later |
| Max price I'd pay | number | Yes | Becomes target price in watchlist |

**CTA button:** "Alert Me When Prices Drop"

**Flow:**
1. User enters email + target price
2. Backend: create newsletter subscriber with `source="world-cup-price-alert"` + store price intent
3. Show success: "We'll email you when World Cup tickets drop below $[price]. Want more control? [Create a free account →](/register?email=[prefilled])"
4. The pre-filled email on the registration page reduces friction for users who convert to full accounts

**Why this matters:**
- Converts the 4-step process to 1 step
- Captures email without requiring full registration
- The target price input creates psychological commitment ("I've already told them my budget")
- `source` attribution enables measuring World Cup page conversion independently
- Pre-filled email on `/register` creates a warm handoff from alert subscriber to registered user

### Form Scorecard (Proposed)

| Criterion                  | Score | Notes                                    |
|---------------------------|-------|------------------------------------------|
| Field count               | ✅ 2  | Email + target price — minimal           |
| CTA copy                  | ✅    | "Alert Me When Prices Drop" — action-oriented, benefit-clear |
| Value reinforcement       | ✅    | Immediate promise of a specific outcome  |
| Post-submit flow          | ✅    | Upsell to full account with pre-filled email |
| Friction                  | ✅    | No password, no confirm, no registration |
| Placement coverage        | ✅    | World Cup landing + stadium pages        |
| Source attribution        | ✅    | `source="world-cup-price-alert"`         |
| Mobile layout             | ✅    | Inline, stacks naturally                 |
