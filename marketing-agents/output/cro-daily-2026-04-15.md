## Form Friction Check: Newsletter Popup

**Today's form:** Newsletter signup card variant (`NewsletterSignup.tsx`, card variant, lines 114-155)

This is the primary newsletter capture on the homepage (`source="homepage"`).

| Aspect | Current | Issue | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Headline | "Never Miss a Deal" | Generic — could be any deals site | "Get Price Drop Alerts" — specific to ticket tracking value prop |
| Body copy | "Get exclusive ticket deals, price drop alerts, and tips..." | Lists 3 things; dilutes focus | Lead with the unique value: "We'll email you when prices drop on events you care about." |

| Success state | Green confirmation message | Adequate | Add a next step: "Want alerts for specific events? Create a free account" — bridges newsletter to registration |
| Placement | Below testimonials, above footer | Visible but below fold | Consider testing inline variant earlier in the page (after "How it Works" section) |

**Key recommendation:** The success state is a missed conversion opportunity. After someone subscribes to the newsletter, they've shown high intent. This bridges the newsletter signup to user registration without additional friction.

**Quick win:** Change button copy from "Subscribe" to "Get Price Alerts" — more specific, reinforces what the user is actually getting. Estimated +5-10% click-through improvement based on CTA specificity studies.
