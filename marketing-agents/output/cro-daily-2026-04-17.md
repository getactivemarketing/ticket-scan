This is excellent SEO work by whoever shipped it. But it raises a structural concern for CRO:

> **We are investing heavily in driving SEO traffic to venue pages while leaving the conversion architecture on those pages untouched.** Every additional keyword, every expanded seating chart, every enriched description is sending more organic users to pages that still have zero newsletter capture, generic CTAs, no social proof, and a context-stripping "Track Price" → `/register` hand-off.

The SEO pipeline is producing. The conversion pipeline is leaking. Today's report re-ranks the existing venue-page P0s with stronger urgency and adds one new P0 tied to the enriched seating-chart data.

### P0 (carried from 04-10, Day 7 open)

1. **No newsletter/email capture anywhere on venue pages.**
   `NewsletterSignup.tsx` exists and is live on the homepage and footer. Venue pages have zero. Cold organic traffic landing on MSG, TD Garden, or SoFi Stadium has no low-commitment conversion path — the only CTAs are "Track Price" and "Start Free," both of which route to account creation. For users not ready to register, there is no second-best option. They bounce.
   **Fix:** Insert `<NewsletterSignup source="venue-{slug}" variant="card" />` between the events list and the "About {venue.name}" SEO content block (around line 220). Copy: "Get price alerts for {venue.name} events." Subtext: "One email when prices drop. No account required."
   **Effort:** 30 min. **Impact:** Estimated 5–10% email capture on cold venue traffic. This is the single highest-leverage unshipped change on the site.

2. **"Track Price" routes to `/register` with zero context.**
   `web/src/app/venues/[slug]/page.tsx:199` — the button links to `/register` as a bare URL. A user who just clicked on "Magic vs Celtics — $45" lands on a blank registration form that says "Start tracking ticket prices today." The event, venue, and price that earned their click are all gone. Classic momentum-killer.
   **Fix:** Pass context via query params: `/register?event={encodeURIComponent(event.name)}&venue={slug}&price={event.minPrice}&source=venue`. On the register page, read these params and render a contextual banner above the form: "You're about to track **{eventName}** at **{venueName}** (from ${price}). Create a free account in 30 seconds."
   **Effort:** 1.5 hrs (link change + register page consumer). **Impact:** Estimated +20–40% lift on venue→register conversion. This is the tightest intent-to-form gap on the site.

3. **No social proof on venue pages.**
   Homepage has `StatsBar` and testimonials. Venue pages — which receive the majority of *cold* organic traffic — have zero trust signals. First-time visitors arriving from Google have no reason to trust the brand, yet the CTA asks for email + password + password.
   **Fix:** Add a compact one-line proof bar below the hero: "Tracking 60+ ticket sites • Avg savings $127 per ticket • Free forever — no credit card." Re-use the homepage `StatsBar` pattern; no new component needed.
   **Effort:** 45 min. **Impact:** Trust precedes conversion. Estimated 10–15% lift on downstream CTAs.

### P0 (new today) — Seating chart enrichment has no conversion surface

A user who comes in from a "TD Garden seating chart" search — one of the newly-added keywords — gets *less* information than the underlying data already supports. **Effort:** 3–4 hrs. **Impact:** Converts pure SEO-content surface area into a funnel entry point. Without this, the new seating data is pure load on the page for no conversion return.

### Critical Issues

**P0 — No source attribution.** Every upstream agent (SEO, Email, Content, Ads) is shipping campaigns that link to `/register`. We have no way to measure which source converts. Implement:
```tsx
// register/page.tsx
const searchParams = useSearchParams();
const source = searchParams.get('source'); // 'venue' | 'compare' | 'email-drip-7' | 'wc-2026' | etc.
// Pass to register() and persist on user record for cohort analysis
```
Zero-cost change. Would immediately tell us which $X of SEO/email traffic converts at what rate.
**Effort:** 1 hour (incl. backend column on `users` table). **Impact:** Unlocks attribution for *every other CRO decision going forward.*

**P0 — No source-aware value reinforcement.** Tied to the venue-page P0 above. When a user arrives from a specific event, show:
> "You're one step from tracking **Magic vs Celtics** at TD Garden (from $45). We'll email you when the price drops."
Instead of the current:
> "Start tracking ticket prices today."
The specificity is the conversion lever. Generic headlines lose 15–25% vs. contextual ones in almost every study on record.

**P1 — Drop the confirm-password field.**
The confirm-password pattern has been industry-discredited for ~8 years. Replace with a show/hide eye icon on a single password field. Google, GitHub, Stripe, and Shopify all dropped it. Conversion lift: 5–10%. Typo prevention: equal or better. One less field = one less cognitive load.
**Effort:** 20 min.

**P1 — Inline validation.**
Password match and length should validate on blur/change, not only on submit. A user who typed two mismatched passwords learns *after* pressing the submit button; this feels like a scolding. Show a green check on "passwords match" as they type the second one.

**P1 — No value reinforcement above the form.**
Right now the only pre-form content is "Create your account" + "Start tracking ticket prices today." Add a 3-bullet strip:
- "Track any event — no credit card"
- "Compare Ticketmaster, SeatGeek & StubHub"
- "We'll email you when prices drop"
Copy is context-dependent — see P0 #2 when source is known.

**P2 — Error message sanitization.**
`err.message` may surface raw API or DB strings. Wrap with a dictionary:
```ts
const userFacingError = err.message.includes('already') 
  ? 'An account with this email exists. Sign in instead.'
  : err.message.includes('invalid')
  ? 'That email address looks off — please check it.'
  : 'Something went wrong. Please try again.';
```
And for the "already exists" case, show a `→ Sign in` link inline.

**P2 — Post-registration destination.**
Currently: unconditional `router.push('/dashboard')`. Should be:
- If `source=venue&slug=msg` → redirect to `/venues/msg` with a toast "Account created. Now tracking MSG events."
- If `source=compare&q=taylor-swift` → redirect to the compare query they were trying to view.
- Default: `/dashboard`.
This closes the loop on P0 #2 (context preservation).

### Recommended A/B test on Register

**Hypothesis:** Removing confirm-password and adding a source-aware contextual headline will lift registration completion rate by ≥20%.
- Variant A (control): Current 3-field form, generic headline.
- Variant B: 2-field form (email + password with show/hide), contextual headline pulled from `?source=` and `?event=` params, 3-bullet value strip.
- **Primary metric:** Registration completion rate (form view → account created).
- **Secondary:** Day-1 watchlist add rate (do contextual-entry users add more?).
- **Duration:** 2 weeks or until statistical significance.
