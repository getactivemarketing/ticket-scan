The Apr 25 fix-spec for the WC newsletter card included the line `Tournament starts in 47 days` as the eyebrow above the headline. **Seven days later it would say `40 days`** — a number that crosses the "this is happening soon" threshold (under 6 weeks). Marketing psychology research consistently shows urgency framing converts higher inside the 30–60 day window than at >60 days, and the slope steepens further at <30. **Every day this ships later is a day of suboptimal urgency framing on the largest fixed-deadline surface on the platform.**

The fix is the same fix from Apr 25 (newsletter card mid-page on main + sidebar block on stadium). The new finding is the *cost-of-delay analytic* on top of it: ship-by date matters here in a way it does not for the rest of the backlog. **Recommendation: this jumps to the top of the dev queue this week, ahead of the venue-page work,** because the venue-page work is evergreen and the WC capture work has a hard expiry.
