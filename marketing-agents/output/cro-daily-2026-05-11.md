## Form Rotation: Newsletter popup — Day 13 since first spec'd

**The audit subject today is the absence.** The task brief lists "Newsletter popup" as one of the 5 forms to rotate through. The 5/3 audit observed the popup does not exist; today's audit, 13 days later, re-confirms: `Grep` for `NewsletterPopup`, `newsletter-popup`, `popup` across `web/src/` returns **zero matches** — there is no popup component, no exit-intent handler, no time-on-page modal trigger, no scroll-depth modal trigger, no `useEffect` watching for visibility-change to show a modal, no localStorage-keyed dismissal logic. **The most conventional conversion-rate-optimization surface on a content-heavy marketing site does not exist on TicketScan.**

### What "should" exist (the spec the audit cycle has been carrying)

A newsletter popup component with these behaviors:
- **Trigger:** Exit-intent on desktop (mouse leaves viewport top) + time-on-page ≥30s + scroll-depth ≥40% on mobile (no exit-intent on touch).
- **Dismissal:** "No thanks" button + ESC key + click-outside + localStorage flag (`newsletter_popup_dismissed_at` timestamp; re-show after 14 days).
- **Composition:** Re-uses the existing `<NewsletterSignup variant="card" source="popup-{trigger_type}">` (so source attribution distinguishes exit-intent vs. time-on-page vs. scroll-depth captures).
- **Telemetry:** `dataLayer.push({event: 'newsletter_popup_shown', trigger})` + `dataLayer.push({event: 'newsletter_popup_dismissed', method})` + the existing newsletter_subscribed push fires on success.
- **Routes:** Mount globally from `app/layout.tsx`. Disable on `/register`, `/login`, `/admin/*`, `/dashboard` (these surfaces have their own conversion logic).
- **A11y:** Focus trap, `role="dialog"`, `aria-labelledby` on the heading, return focus to trigger element on dismiss.
