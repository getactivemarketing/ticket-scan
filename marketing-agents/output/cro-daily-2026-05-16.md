### Popup spec (today's form rotation = de-facto design spec, as 5/13 was for the watchlist add modal)

**Component:** `web/src/components/NewsletterPopup.tsx` (new file)

**Behavior:**
- Mount globally in `layout.tsx` next to `<Footer />`
- Trigger on **scroll past 50% of viewport height** OR **30 seconds elapsed**, whichever comes first
- Trigger only **once per session** (`sessionStorage.setItem('ts_popup_shown', '1')`)
- Trigger only on **routes matching `/world-cup-2026.*`, `/venues/.*`, `/blog/.*`, `/tickets/.*`** (high-cold-traffic SEO surfaces) for the WC window; expand to homepage + dashboard after the cycle.
- Suppress on `/register`, `/login`, `/watchlist`, `/admin`, `/event/.*` (in-flow pages where the popup would interrupt conversion).
- Dismissible via X (top-right), Escape key, click outside modal.
- Dismissed-state persists for **30 days** (`localStorage.setItem('ts_popup_dismissed_at', Date.now())`) to avoid creep on returning visitors.

**Form (1 field):**
- `<input type="email" autoComplete="email" inputMode="email" required>`
- Submit → `POST /api/newsletter/subscribe` with `{email, source: 'popup-' + pageType}` where `pageType` is one of `wc-main | wc-stadium-${slug} | venue-${slug} | blog-${slug} | city-${slug}`.
- Success state: replace form with "Thanks! We'll email you the best WC ticket deals." + a single `<Link href="/register?...">` CTA labeled "Create a free account for instant alerts" (with the auth-boundary cluster's `subheadBySource` map updated to include `popup-followup-${pageType}` keys).
