### What's new today on the compare page (Day +14 from 5/5)

#### P0 (NEW today) — **The compare page is a Google-indexed bait-and-switch: SEO metadata at `compare/layout.tsx:4–26` claims a free public price-comparison tool; cold traffic landing the page sees a blank screen before redirecting to `/login`.**

`compare/layout.tsx:3–7` exports a `Metadata` with `title: 'Compare Ticket Prices — Ticketmaster vs SeatGeek Side-by-Side'` and `description: 'Compare ticket prices side-by-side from Ticketmaster and SeatGeek...'`. The JSON-LD `WebApplication` at `:32–62` declares `offers.price: '0'` (free), `applicationCategory: 'BusinessApplication'`, and a `featureList` of 5 capabilities. **Google's crawler indexes this metadata and surfaces the page in SERPs for "compare ticket prices" and adjacent intent queries.**

Cold visitor flow:
1. Searches "compare ticket prices ticketmaster vs seatgeek"
2. Clicks the `/compare` result
3. Page renders briefly (React hydrates, `authLoading: true`)
4. `authLoading` flips to `false`, `user` is `null`, `compare/page.tsx:87` evaluates true
5. `router.push('/login')` fires
6. User sees a blank screen flash, then the login form

**Google's quality guidelines explicitly flag this pattern as deceptive.** The relevant Search Central documentation: "Don't deceive your users." A free tool that requires registration is allowed; a free tool that requires registration *before letting the user even attempt to use the search form* is harder to defend. **There is a non-zero probability that Google has already demoted this page** — the `/compare` URL would need a Search Console impressions/CTR check to confirm.

**Effort:** the soft-gate refactor (C1 above, 20 min) resolves this entirely. The search form should render for logged-out users; the gate moves to the "save this comparison" / "set alert" affordance level. **Cost-of-delay: Day 35 of bait-and-switch SEO. The compare page is the most directly-targetable SEO surface on the platform for transactional buyer queries; every day it's behind a hard-gate, organic CTR decays and Google's ranking signals worsen.**

**Composes with:** C1, C2 (returnTo restoration), C10 (alert affordance gating). The single 30-min commit resolves the structural defect that has the highest cost-of-delay on the entire platform.
