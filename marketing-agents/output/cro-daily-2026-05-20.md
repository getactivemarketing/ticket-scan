### What's new today on the dashboard (Day +21 from 5/4)

#### P0 (NEW today) — **The dashboard search submits the form with City `'Orlando'` (default) and ALL other fields empty, and the backend response will return real Orlando events — meaning a non-Orlando user who clicks "Search Events" before changing the city gets *Orlando results they did not ask for*, with no indication that the city was inferred (not chosen).**

The flow:
1. Non-Orlando user lands on `/dashboard` (e.g., from a Brooklyn-related blog post)
2. Sees the search form with "City: Orlando" pre-filled
3. Either (a) doesn't notice the pre-fill, clicks Search → gets Orlando results, bounces ("this site doesn't have my city"), OR (b) notices the pre-fill, clears it, types Brooklyn, clicks Search

**The (a) path is invisible in logs as a defect** — it looks like a successful search. But the user perceives the platform as broken/irrelevant and leaves. The (b) path adds 2–3 seconds of friction that compounds across the cohort.

**Cohort impact estimate:** of the 24 venues, 37 cities, and 17 WC stadium pages indexed, **2 are in Orlando** (Amway Center, Camping World Stadium) and **the remaining ~76 SEO pages route non-Orlando cold traffic to a dashboard that pre-fills Orlando.** That is, **~97% of SEO-routed search starts are pre-filled with the wrong city.** This composes directly with the 72-hour-flat watchlist counter: registered users from non-Orlando cohorts who never see a relevant event will never add one to their watchlist.

**Effort:** 2 min (D1 above). The fix is a 1-character change (`'Orlando'` → `''`).

**Cost-of-delay framing:** at conservative assumption that 50% of new registrations route through `/dashboard` and 80% of those are non-Orlando, the platform is failing ~40% of new users in their first 5 seconds on the dashboard. **The 72-hour-flat watchlist counter is the empirical signature of this failure.**
