## Page-Level Review — World Cup page (Saturday rotation)

`/world-cup-2026` is the campaign's flagship surface in the literal launch window (T-5). It is **well-built for SEO**: `SportsEvent` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD (`:99–106`), strong keyword metadata (`:6–21`), a clear hero with a *low-friction* browse CTA ("Search World Cup Tickets" → `/dashboard?keyword=...`, `:122–127`), value props, host-city grids linking to 16 stadium guides, key dates, and a tips section. The hero's browse-first CTA is genuinely good — it lets a cold visitor explore without an account wall.

The problems are (1) the same missing-light-step conversion gap as every other landing surface, and (2) a timeliness regression in the copy.
