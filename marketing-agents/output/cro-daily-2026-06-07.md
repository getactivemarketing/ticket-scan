## Page-Level Review — Newsletter/Blog Conversion Path

Verified against source today. The working `<NewsletterSignup>` form (POSTs to
`/api/newsletter/subscribe`) existed in **only one place: the homepage**. Every
other newsletter touchpoint was a **dead-end "Subscribe" button that linked to
`/`** — dumping the user at the top of the homepage to hunt for the form. That
is the mechanical reason blog/footer traffic converted at ~0.

| Surface | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `/blog` index CTA | `<Link href="/">Subscribe Now` (dead end) | Inline `<NewsletterSignup source="blog-index">` form |
| `/blog/[slug]` sidebar | `<Link href="/">Subscribe` (dead end) | `<NewsletterSignup source="blog-post" variant="footer">` form |
| Global `Footer.tsx` (every page) | **No newsletter capture at all** | `<NewsletterSignup source="site-footer" variant="footer">` form |

The component already shipped a `footer` variant that was never mounted globally —
so this was wiring existing, tested code, not new surface area. Per-surface
`source` values are distinct so subscriptions can be attributed by origin in the
`newsletter_subscribers.source` column (the one piece of attribution we *do* have).

**Shipped (working tree, `web/`):**
- `src/app/blog/page.tsx` — real form replaces dead-end CTA
- `src/app/blog/[slug]/page.tsx` — real form replaces dead-end CTA
- `src/components/Footer.tsx` — newsletter capture added above the link grid, on every page

**Verification:** `npx tsc --noEmit` → exit 0, no errors.
