## Page Review — World Cup landing (`web/src/app/world-cup-2026/page.tsx`)

Cold-traffic SEO page; high-intent seasonal traffic (tournament is live now, June 11–July 19). Strengths first: hero CTAs are **not** login-gated — "Search World Cup Tickets" → `/dashboard` (`:126`) and "View Match Schedule" → `#schedule` (`:132`) both let anon visitors act. Good. Three real friction points:

**P1 (highest leverage) — No email capture at peak intent.**
The page builds intent through tips + venue guides, then the closing CTA (`:391–415`) offers only "Create Free Account" (`/register`) and "Search Tickets Now". The *only* no-account capture is the footer `NewsletterSignup`, below everything. A cold World Cup visitor who isn't ready to register has no low-friction way to stay engaged at the moment they're most warmed up.
→ **Recommendation:** Add `NewsletterSignup variant="inline"` inside/just above the closing CTA block, framed "Get World Cup price-drop alerts — no account needed." Same pattern already shipped on blog/footer. *(Dev backlog — small, additive. Mirrors venue-page P1.)*

**P2 — Stale trust copy on a trust-dependent page.**
Two lines read as out-of-date now that the tournament is live (today is 2026-06-13):
- `:344` Final "Location TBA (likely SoFi or MetLife)" — the host of the final has since been decided; "TBA" undermines credibility.
- `:363` "Official FIFA ticket sales **will begin in late 2025**" — written future-tense for a date now ~6 months past.
On a page where trust *is* conversion, stale facts cost signups.
→ **Content Agent (Agent 1):** verify current final venue + rewrite tense to present/live-tournament. *(I'm not asserting the corrected fixtures here — `worldcup.ts` is known-stale/pre-draw; needs real-source verification before edit.)*

**P3 — Social proof is qualitative only.**
"Save Hundreds / 30%+ / Trusted Platforms" (`:162–190`) make claims with no concrete proof point. Low priority. (Note: with only 115 users / 3 subscribers, a user-count badge would *hurt* right now — skip until counts are stronger; a "prices compared across 3 platforms" framing is safer.)

## Form Review — Registration (`web/src/app/register/page.tsx`)

Picked because **every** World Cup conversion CTA funnels here. Decent baseline; concrete gaps:

- **Field count: 3** (email, password, confirm password). The confirm-password field is the removable one — modern pattern is a single password with a show/hide toggle, cutting fields by 33%. → **Drop confirm-password, add show/hide toggle.** Biggest friction win on this form.
- **No `autoComplete` attributes** (`:69–107`). Add `autoComplete="email"` and `autoComplete="new-password"` so browsers/password managers prefill — measurable mobile completion lift, one-line each.
- **Email `type="email"` ✅** (`:71`) — triggers email keyboard on mobile.
- **Loading state ✅** — button shows "Creating account..." (`:115`).
- **Inline validation ⚠️** — password match/length only checked on submit (`:22–30`). Fine, but surfacing the 8-char rule as helper text *under* the field (not just on error) prevents the failed-submit round-trip.
- **Reassurance missing:** no "free, no credit card" microcopy near the button. Subhead says "Start tracking ticket prices today" (`:54`) — good, but add a one-line free/no-card note to lower commitment fear. *(→ Content Agent for wording.)*
