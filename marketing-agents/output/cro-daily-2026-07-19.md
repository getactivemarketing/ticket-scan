
A visitor landing on the MetLife stadium page today via "World Cup Final tickets" search sees "World Cup 2026 Host Stadium" — which tells them nothing they didn't know. The hero has no signal that the Final is *today* at this specific venue. The word "Final" does not appear in the hero section at all.

**Fix:** The stadium page currently renders a static subtitle. For MetLife specifically (the Final venue), add a conditional urgency override. The slug for MetLife is `metlife-stadium` — check `venue.slug` and inject a Final-day label:

```tsx

  World Cup 2026 Host Stadium

```

Or more simply, add the Final designation to the MetLife venue data in `worldcup.ts` so the page template picks it up without a special case.
