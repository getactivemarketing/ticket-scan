### Page-Level Conversion Review — Homepage

**What works**

- The value proposition is understandable within five seconds: “Compare. Track. Save.” followed by multi-platform price comparison and drop alerts.
- “Get Started Free” is visible above the fold and has a clear destination (`/register`). “See How It Works” provides a lower-commitment path.
- The page includes repeated paths to registration, comparison/search via browse content, social proof-style testimonials, FAQ reassurance, and a final account CTA.
- Production homepage smoke check returned HTTP 200 in approximately 0.50s from the audit location. This is server response time, not Core Web Vitals or a mobile Lighthouse result.

**Recommendations**

1. **P1 — Remove the homepage’s duplicate custom footer or stop rendering the global footer on this route.** Keep one footer and one clearly attributed footer newsletter form. This reduces duplicate navigation, prevents competing signup placements, and makes source-level conversion reporting trustworthy.
2. **P1 — Add a comparison-first CTA beside the registration CTA.** Use `Compare prices free` linking to `/compare` (or `/dashboard` if comparison requires search), with `Get price-drop alerts` as the account path. The current above-fold CTA asks for registration before demonstrating the core product.
3. **P1 — Repair or qualify the real-time/alert promise.** Until price tracking and alert reporting are healthy, either restore those systems or change supporting copy to avoid promising monitoring the product cannot currently prove.
4. **P2 — Replace unverified proof with measurable proof.** “50K+ Events Tracked,” “$127 Avg. Savings,” and “Join thousands of fans” are static claims with no visible source or date. Add a last-updated date and methodology, or use a verifiable marketplace comparison example.

**Mobile review:** Hero buttons stack appropriately. The homepage card and inline newsletter forms stack on small screens, but the footer variant remains a horizontal flex row and may squeeze the email field on narrow devices. Validate at 320px/375px with a real-device or Lighthouse run.

### Form Friction Check — Newsletter Signup

- **Fields:** One email field; good. Do not add name, preferences, or password fields.
- **Input semantics:** `type="email"` and `required` are correct. Add an explicit visually hidden `<label>`, `autoComplete="email"`, `inputMode="email"`, and `aria-describedby` for the cadence/privacy expectation.
- **Copy:** “Subscribe” and “Get Deals” are generic. Test `Get price alerts` or `Send me ticket deals`, tied to a concrete offer.
- **Loading state:** The footer shows `...`, which is weak feedback. Use `Joining…` or `Saving…` consistently across variants.
- **Error/success feedback:** Messages are rendered visually but lack `aria-live`; add a polite status region and distinguish validation, duplicate subscription, and API failure.
- **Trust/cadence:** “No spam, unsubscribe anytime.” is useful. Add an explicit cadence such as `1–2 emails/week · price drops, fee math, and smarter buy timing.`
- **Popup:** No newsletter popup component or popup events were found. Popup conversion is not applicable, not zero.
- **Instrumentation:** Emit `newsletter_viewed`, `newsletter_started`, `newsletter_submitted`, `newsletter_success`, and `newsletter_failed` with `source`, `placement`, `page`, and error category. Never send the email address.
