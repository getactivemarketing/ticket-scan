### Page-Level Conversion Review — Blog Path

**What works**

- `/blog` has a clear headline and benefit statement: ticket-buying advice, comparison guidance, and saving money.
- Blog posts include a strong end-of-article account CTA (`Create Free Account`) plus quick links to search, compare, and account creation.
- The blog index includes a dedicated newsletter card after the article list; article pages include a newsletter card in the sidebar.
- Live smoke checks returned HTTP 200. Server response was approximately 0.39s for `/blog` and 0.32s for the homepage from this audit location. This is not a Core Web Vitals or mobile performance result.

**Recommendations**

1. **P1 — Put the newsletter promise closer to the reading decision.** On article pages, the newsletter form is in the sidebar after the main article in the mobile stacking order, so mobile readers reach it only after the full article. Add one compact inline capture after the introduction or first major section, using the article topic as the source/context.
2. **P1 — Make the primary article CTA outcome-specific.** Replace `Create Free Account` with `Track this event and get price alerts` when the article discusses a specific event, or `Compare ticket prices free` for general guides. Preserve the current register CTA as the secondary path.
3. **P1 — Reduce competing capture surfaces and make placement attribution explicit.** A blog post can expose sidebar newsletter, global footer newsletter, account CTA, and related links. Keep the inline newsletter plus one footer fallback, and instrument each with a stable placement/source value so conversion rates can be compared.

**Mobile check:** The blog-post grid stacks the article before the sidebar, making the sidebar newsletter late in the mobile journey. Newsletter footer fields use a horizontal flex row and should be tested at 320px and 375px; the email field may be squeezed beside the button.

### Form Friction Check — Newsletter Signup

- **Fields:** One email field; good. Keep it to one field.
- **Semantics:** `type="email"` and `required` are correct. Add a visible or visually hidden label, `autoComplete="email"`, `inputMode="email"`, and `aria-describedby` for cadence/privacy copy.
- **Copy:** `Subscribe` and `Get Deals` are generic and inconsistent. Test **Get price alerts** or **Send me ticket deals**, tied to a concrete benefit.
- **Loading state:** The footer variant displays `...`, which does not explain what is happening. Use **Joining…** consistently across variants.
- **Errors/success:** Messages are visible but lack `aria-live`. Add a polite status region and distinguish invalid email, already subscribed, API failure, and network failure. The API already returns a successful “already subscribed” response, so preserve that as a non-error state.
- **Trust/cadence:** `No spam, unsubscribe anytime.` is useful but incomplete. Add `1–2 emails/week · price drops, useful ticket math, and smarter buy timing.`
- **Popup:** No newsletter popup component or popup exposure events were found. Popup conversion is not applicable, not zero.
- **Instrumentation:** Emit `newsletter_viewed`, `newsletter_started`, `newsletter_submitted`, `newsletter_success`, and `newsletter_failed` with `source`, `placement`, `page`, and error category. Never send the email address.
