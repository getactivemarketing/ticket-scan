### Page-Level Conversion Review — Blog Path

**What works**

- `/blog` has a clear value proposition: practical advice on finding better ticket deals, timing purchases, and comparing prices.
- The index presents featured and regular articles with readable titles, summaries, read times, and “Read More” links.
- The blog index has a dedicated newsletter card after the article list. Individual articles include a product CTA, search/compare/account links, related content, and a newsletter footer fallback.
- Live smoke checks returned HTTP 200 for `/` and `/blog`; observed server response was approximately 385ms and 182ms respectively from this audit location. This is not a Core Web Vitals or mobile performance measurement.

**Recommendations**

1. **P1 — Move newsletter capture into the reading journey.** Add one compact inline form after the introduction or first major section of each article. The current index card comes after the full article list, and the article footer/sidebar form is late in the mobile journey.
2. **P1 — Make article CTAs outcome-specific.** Use **Compare ticket prices free** for general buying guides and **Track this event and get price alerts** for event-specific content. Keep account creation as the secondary route where appropriate.
3. **P1 — Instrument and simplify the conversion path.** Give every newsletter placement a stable `source`/`placement` value and measure view → start → submit → success. Retain one footer fallback; do not add a popup until baseline behavior is measurable.

**Mobile check:** The blog index is responsive, but newsletter card and footer variants should be tested at 320px and 375px. The footer form uses a horizontal flex row, which can squeeze the email field and button on narrow screens.

### Form Friction Check — Newsletter Signup

- **Fields:** One email field; good. Keep it to one field.
- **Semantics:** `type="email"` and `required` are correct. Add a visible or visually hidden label, `autoComplete="email"`, `inputMode="email"`, and `aria-describedby` for privacy/cadence copy.
- **Copy:** Button copy varies between `Subscribe` and `Get Deals`. Test **Get price alerts** or **Send me ticket deals**, tied to the concrete benefit.
- **Loading state:** Footer uses `...`, while other variants use `Subscribing...`. Use a consistent **Joining…** label and preserve disabled state.
- **Errors/success:** Add `aria-live="polite"`; distinguish invalid email, already subscribed, API failure, and network failure. The successful duplicate-subscription response should remain a non-error state.
- **Trust/cadence:** Keep “No spam, unsubscribe anytime.” and add **1–2 emails/week · price drops, useful ticket math, and smarter buy timing.**
- **Popup:** No newsletter popup component or popup exposure events were found. Popup conversion is not applicable, not zero.
- **Privacy:** Do not send email addresses in analytics events; send placement, page, and error category only.
