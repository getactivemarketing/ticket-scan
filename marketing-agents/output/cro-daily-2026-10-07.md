### Form Friction Review — Newsletter Popup

**Status: Not implemented / cannot review as a live form.** No popup component, trigger, or popup-specific `NewsletterSignup` usage was found. Existing newsletter forms are footer, inline, and card variants.

If a popup is intentionally added later, use this minimum spec:

- One required field: email only; use `type="email"`, `name="email"`, `autoComplete="email"`, and `inputMode="email"`.
- Give it an associated visible or visually hidden label, a clear close button, escape-key support, focus trapping/restoration, and no forced repeat display after dismissal.
- Use supported copy: **“Get verified onsale and presale dates, venue guides, and practical ticket-buying tips.”** Avoid price-drop, price-history, target-price, buy-timing, or TicketScan price-statistic promises.
- Submit copy: **“Get Onsale Updates.”** Loading copy should say **“Joining…”**, not `...`.
- Announce loading, success, duplicate, and error states with `aria-live`/appropriate alert semantics; offer a next step such as **“Browse upcoming onsales.”**
- Instrument `newsletter_popup_view`, `newsletter_popup_submit`, `newsletter_popup_success`, `newsletter_popup_duplicate`, `newsletter_popup_error`, and `newsletter_popup_dismissed` with placement and page path only. Never send the raw email address.
