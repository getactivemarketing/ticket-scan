### Recommendations

1. **P1 — Move a compact newsletter CTA above the article inventory.** Place a one-field inline signup directly below the hero, with copy tied to the actual supported value: onsale/presale dates, venue guides, and buying tips. Keep the full card after the articles as a secondary conversion point.

3. **P1 — Replace generic “Read More” with context-preserving CTA text.** Use the article category or outcome in the link text, such as “Read the venue guide” or “See the buying checklist,” while keeping the title as the accessible link heading. This gives users a clearer next action without adding fields.

4. **P1 — Add an event-relevant next step to article pages.** After the article’s first useful section, add a supported CTA to browse events or onsale dates. Avoid price-alert or price-history language until the tracking system is repaired.

## Form Friction Review — Newsletter Signup

### Findings

- One required email field is appropriately minimal and uses `type="email"`.
- The input has no associated visible or visually-hidden `<label>`, no `name`, and no `autoComplete="email"`.
- Loading copy is inconsistent by variant (`...` in the footer, `Subscribing...` in card/inline) and does not communicate a clear state in the footer.
- Success and error messages are not exposed with `role="status"`, `role="alert"`, or an `aria-live` region.
- Errors are rendered below the form but are not associated with the input through `aria-describedby`.
- The form has no first-party events for viewed, submit started, success, duplicate, or error; raw email must not be included in any event payload.
- CTA language is inconsistent: `Subscribe` in the card/footer and `Get Alerts` in the inline variant. “Get Alerts” is risky while price-alert functionality is unavailable.

### Recommended form spec — P1

```text
Label: Email address
Supporting copy: Onsale dates, presales, venue guides, and useful ticket-buying tips.
Field: type=email, name=email, autoComplete=email, inputMode=email
Button: Get onsale updates
Loading: Joining…
Success: You’re on the list. We’ll send useful ticket updates, not inbox confetti.
Error: Enter a valid email address, or try again in a moment.
```

Add an associated label, `aria-describedby`, live status/error regions, and explicit form events: `newsletter_form_viewed`, `newsletter_submit_started`, `newsletter_subscribed`, `newsletter_duplicate`, and `newsletter_error`. Include `source` and page path, never the email address.
