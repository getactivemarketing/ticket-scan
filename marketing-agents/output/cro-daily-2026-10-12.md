## Form Friction Review — Homepage Newsletter Signup

### Findings

- One required email field is appropriately minimal and uses `type="email"`.
- The input has no associated label, `name`, `autoComplete="email"`, or explicit `inputMode`.
- The card form uses generic “Subscribe” copy; the supporting copy promises alerts and should be narrowed to verified onsale/presale updates, venue guides, and tips.
- Loading, success, and error messages are not exposed through `aria-live`, `role="status"`, or `role="alert"`; error text is not associated with the input through `aria-describedby`.
- No first-party lifecycle events distinguish form view, submit, success, duplicate, or error. The API receives `source="homepage"`, but the source is not available in a CRO report.
- The one-field form is otherwise short, clear, and suitable for mobile.

### Recommended form spec — P1

```text
Label: Email address
Supporting copy: Onsale dates, presales, venue guides, and useful ticket-buying tips.
Field: type=email, name=email, autoComplete=email, inputMode=email
Button: Get onsale updates
Loading: Joining…
Success: You’re on the list. We’ll send useful ticket updates, not inbox confetti.
Error: Enter a valid email address, or try again in a moment.
```

Add an associated label, `aria-describedby`, live status/error regions, and events named `newsletter_form_viewed`, `newsletter_submit_started`, `newsletter_subscribed`, `newsletter_duplicate`, and `newsletter_error`. Include placement and page path, never the email address.
