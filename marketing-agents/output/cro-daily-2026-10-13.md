## Form Friction Review — Compare Search Form

### Findings

- Four inputs are labeled with `htmlFor`/`id`, and the date fields use native `type="date"`, which is good for mobile entry.
- City and event are both free-text and neither is required. An empty keyword may be valid for a broad city search, but the form does not explain that behavior.
- The default city is hidden in state rather than communicated as a search example or selectable suggestion; users may submit Orlando unintentionally.
- The submit button correctly disables during loading and changes to “Searching…”.
- The error container is visually styled but lacks `role="alert"` in the compare page source, so screen-reader users may not hear failures.
- There is no inline validation for an end date earlier than the start date beyond the HTML `min` attribute, no field-level error association, and no submit/success/result instrumentation.
- The form does not ask for unnecessary personal data. Registration is currently imposed at page entry rather than at the point where saved functionality is requested.

### Recommended form spec — P0/P1

```text
Heading: Compare event listings
Supporting copy: Match the same date and venue across available seller listings.
City label: City (required)
Event label: Event, artist, or team (optional)
From label: From date (optional)
To label: To date (optional)
Button: Compare listings
Loading: Searching listings…
Error: We couldn’t load listings. Try again in a moment.
Result CTA: Review seller details
```

Implementation requests:

- Decide whether city is required; if broad search is supported, say so explicitly and keep the default blank or visibly marked as an example.
- Add `role="alert"`/`aria-live="polite"` to error and result-status regions, plus field-level date errors tied through `aria-describedby`.
- Add `autoComplete="address-level2"` to city and appropriate mobile-friendly input metadata.
- Fire `compare_form_viewed`, `compare_submit_started`, `compare_results_loaded`, `compare_empty`, `compare_error`, and `outbound_ticket_click`; include route, result count, source, and UTM context, never raw email or free-text search terms unless the privacy policy explicitly permits it.
- Remove price/savings result copy until current price ingestion passes an end-to-end check.
