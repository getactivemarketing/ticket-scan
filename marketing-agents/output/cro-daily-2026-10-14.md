## Page-Level Conversion Review — Dashboard/Search

### What is working

- The primary action, **Search Events**, is visible without scrolling and has a clear form label.
- The search form uses four relevant filters: city, event/artist/team, from date, and to date.
- Date inputs prevent selecting dates before today and constrain the end date to the start date when supplied.
- The anonymous-user banner makes the next step explicit: create a free account to save events.
- Loading, empty, and error states are present; the loading state uses shaped event-card skeletons rather than a blank spinner.
- The live route returned HTTP 200 in approximately 0.30s from this check.

### Recommendations

1. **P0 — Instrument the funnel before optimizing it.** Add typed events for `dashboard_view`, `search_initiated`, `search_success`, `search_empty`, `signup_start`, `signup_complete`, `watchlist_add`, and outbound seller clicks. Include route, source CTA, result count, and a non-PII session identifier; do not send email addresses.
2. **P1 — Make the search-to-save benefit more concrete.** After results load for an anonymous user, add a compact inline CTA such as “Save this event to your watchlist — free account” beside the result count. Keep the promise limited to saving and revisiting event/onsale details; do not mention price tracking or alerts.
3. **P1 — Reduce the comparison gate.** Test a logged-out preview of listing metadata or a clear explanation of what account creation unlocks before redirecting to `/login`. Measure `compare_view → signup_start → signup_complete` once telemetry exists.
