## Newsletter Form Review

Component: `web/src/components/NewsletterSignup.tsx`, rendered on the homepage and shared footer.

What works:

- One required field; no unnecessary data collection.
- `type="email"` gives mobile users the appropriate keyboard.
- Loading, success, and error states exist.
- The source is passed internally without exposing a second user-facing field.

Recommendations:

- **P1 — make the field machine-readable:** add `name="email"` and `autoComplete="email"` to every variant.
- **P1 — add accessible status semantics:** give success/error feedback an `aria-live="polite"` region and mark the form/button busy while the request is pending.
- **P1 — remove alert ambiguity:** replace inline “Get Alerts” and footer “Get Onsale Alerts” with “Get Onsale Dates & Tips” or “Get Ticket Tips.” Do not imply price-drop or target-price alerts.
- **P2 — use an action-oriented loading label:** replace a bare `...` with “Subscribing…” consistently across variants.
- **P2 — add privacy-safe telemetry after success:** emit `newsletter_success` with only the form source; emit a normalized error class on failure. Never send the email address or raw API response.
