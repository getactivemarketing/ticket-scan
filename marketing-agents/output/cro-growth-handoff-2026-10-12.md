# Growth Agent Handoff — First-Action Onboarding

The current post-registration route goes directly to `/dashboard` with no guided first action. Use `onboarding-flow-2026-10-12.md` as the implementation brief.

The success action is: search for an event, save it, and revisit its event details/seller links. The flow may collect one interest and one city, but must remain skippable and finish in under 60 seconds.

Do not build the onboarding around price alerts, price history, price trends, target prices, or buy/wait recommendations while those features are unavailable.
