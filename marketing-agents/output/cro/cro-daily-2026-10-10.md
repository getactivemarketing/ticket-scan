## Page-Level Conversion Review — `/world-cup-2026`

### Recommendations

1. **P1 — Reframe the page as an archive and venue-guide hub.** Change the H1 to **“World Cup 2026: Host Stadiums, Schedule & Results”** and the primary CTA to **“Browse Match & Stadium Guides”** linking to `#schedule` or the stadium guide section. This matches the completed tournament and the page’s strongest verified content.

2. **P1 — Remove the onsale-alert claim from the ticket tips section.** Replace “Set Onsale Alerts” with **“Save the match details”** and explain that users can revisit the schedule, venue, and seller links. Do not imply a price alert, target alert, buy-timing signal, or unverified email trigger.

3. **P2 — Add a relevant conversion bridge after the schedule.** Use one factual CTA: **“Get verified onsale and presale dates, venue guides, and ticket-buying tips.”** Link to the existing newsletter form or `/onsales`. This is more useful to a post-tournament visitor than sending them to a likely-empty World Cup event search.
