## Page-Level Conversion Review — `/venues/msg`

### Recommendations

1. **P0 — Correct the global tracking language before driving more registrations.** Replace the homepage/register/venue phrasing that implies price monitoring with verified value: “Save events, check onsale dates, and compare seller links when you’re ready.” Keep onsale and presale claims only where the delivery path is confirmed. This is the highest-impact trust fix.

2. **P1 — Make the venue page’s primary action event-specific.** For each upcoming event, use a clear hierarchy such as **“Check availability”** for the seller link and **“Save event”** for the watchlist action. Add a short line near the list: “Prices are shown on the seller site; compare the same section and the all-in total before checkout.” Do not imply TicketScan has current price data.

3. **P1 — Collapse or label non-event inventory.** Group tour experiences under a secondary “Tours” section or hide duplicate dates when the underlying event is the same. Keep sports/music events as the primary conversion surface.

Preserve the originating event/venue in a safe return parameter so a visitor who clicks “Save Event” does not lose intent after registration.
