## Form Friction Review — Newsletter Signup

### Finding

No newsletter popup implementation was found in the reviewed frontend sources. Popup views, starts, dismissals, abandonment, and conversion are therefore **N/A**, not zero.

The available `NewsletterSignup` component has one required field, `type="email"`, loading/success/error states, and no unnecessary fields. Friction and trust issues:

- no visible or programmatically associated label;
- missing `name="email"`, `autoComplete="email"`, and `inputMode="email"`;
- success/error messages are not exposed through a live region;
- generic CTA labels (`Subscribe`, `Get Alerts`) do not state the benefit;
- alert language is not status-safe while email delivery and alert functionality are not verified;
- no events distinguish form view, submit, validation error, API error, duplicate, and success.

### Recommended form spec — P1

```text
Heading: Get onsale dates and useful ticket tips
Supporting copy: Presales, onsale dates, venue guides, and practical buying advice. No inbox confetti.
Label: Email address
Field: type=email, name=email, autoComplete=email, inputMode=email
Button: Get ticket updates
Loading: Joining…
Success: You’re on the list. We’ll send useful ticket updates, not inbox confetti.
Error: Enter a valid email address, or try again in a moment.
```

Add `aria-describedby` for supporting/error text, `aria-live="polite"` for loading/success, `role="alert"` for errors, and non-PII events such as `newsletter_form_viewed`, `newsletter_submit_started`, `newsletter_subscribed`, `newsletter_duplicate`, and `newsletter_error`, with page path and placement only.
