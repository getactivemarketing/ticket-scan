# Feature Launch: Referral MVP — 2026-10-09

## Pre-launch: two weeks

- Ship first-touch referral and UTM persistence.
- Add funnel events and an admin view for referral source, signup, activation, and reward status.
- Create a referral landing state with one promise: share useful event and venue resources with friends.
- Prepare a World Cup venue-guide bundle and an evergreen seating-guide reward.
- QA duplicate, self-referral, privacy, and unsubscribe behavior.
- Prepare one email, one blog draft, and channel-specific social copy.

## Launch day

- Email eligible registered users with a copy-link CTA after delivery and suppression checks pass.
- Publish the referral explainer and link to the verified guide reward.
- Post variants on X, Instagram, and Threads using one canonical UTM URL.
- Monitor referred visit → signup → saved event manually for the first day.

## Post-launch: 14 days

- Review the funnel daily and suppress abnormal referral bursts.
- Interview the first five referred users.
- Test reward framing before increasing incentive value.
- Publish a short FAQ based on real questions.

## Launch gates and metrics

- Attribution survives signup and appears in admin reporting.
- Duplicate and self-referral tests pass.
- Consent and unsubscribe behavior is unchanged.
- 5% share rate, 10% referred-visit signup, 25% referred activation, and viral coefficient above 0.20 are initial targets.
