# Feature Launch: Saved Event Starter Loop

## Feature

After signup, ask for an optional interest and city, route to event search, then guide the user to save one event and review its date, onsale details, venue tips, and seller links. Offer referral sharing only after a successful save.

## Pre-launch — two weeks

- Add and QA typed events: signup, search, event view, saved-event add, saved-event view, seller click, and referral.
- Add a one-screen onboarding state with a visible skip option.
- Prepare a landing section explaining event discovery and saved-event organization.
- Interview five users with two or more saved events.
- Prepare one verified venue guide and one practical buying-checklist article.

## Launch day

- Release to 25% of new accounts; expand after error and funnel checks.
- Email subject: “Your ticket plan starts with one saved event.”
- Publish a help article on planning a purchase with dates, venues, onsale information, and seller checks.
- Run three social variants across X, Instagram, and Threads. Avoid price, savings, alert, and recommendation claims.

## Post-launch — 14 days

- Review signup → first save, first save → saved-page view, saved-page view → seller click, and qualified referral.
- Interview five users who skipped onboarding and five who completed it.
- Remove steps that add time without improving first-save completion.
- Do not call retention impact until last-seen data has a full 30-day window.

## Success criteria

- Primary hypothesis: 20% relative improvement in signup-to-first-save.
- Secondary: 50% of savers open the saved-event page within seven days.
- Guardrails: onboarding under 60 seconds; no increase in signup errors, unsubscribes, or complaints.
