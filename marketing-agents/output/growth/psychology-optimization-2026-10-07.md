## Psychology Optimization — 2026-10-07

**Principle:** Commitment and consistency, supported by endowment framing.

**Touchpoint:** Empty-watchlist state immediately after signup and the first-save confirmation.

**Exact copy/UX change:**

Empty state:

> You’re one search away from a useful ticket plan. Find an event, save it to **your watchlist**, and keep its date, venue, onsale details, and buying links in one place.

First-save confirmation:

> Saved to **your watchlist**. Next step: check the event page for onsale timing, venue sections, and links to buy.

Set **Find an event** as the sole primary CTA, and link the confirmation directly to the saved event. Avoid unavailable price-monitoring and alert promises.

**Why this fits today’s evidence:** The live seven-day cohort is 1 activated user out of 4 (25.0%), leaving 3 measurable activation-gap users. The copy turns signup into one concrete next action and reinforces ownership without manufacturing urgency.

**Expected impact:** Hypothesis: increase signup → first watchlist add. Measure first-save rate, time to first save, and return-to-saved-event rate once funnel/session events exist. No lift is claimed before testing.
