## Psychology Optimization — 2026-10-21

### Principle

**Endowment effect / ownership framing**

### Where to apply it

Homepage and dashboard empty states, plus the event-save confirmation.

### Exact UX change

Use “Your saved events” consistently instead of “Watchlist” as the first-run label. After a save, show:

> Saved to your events. We’ll keep the event details, onsale timing, venue guide, and purchase links in one place.

Primary CTA: **View your saved events**

Empty state:

> Your saved events live here. Find a game, concert, or show to get started.

This frames the list as the user’s useful collection without implying that TicketScan is tracking prices or sending price alerts.

### Expected impact

Hypothesis: clearer ownership language will increase first-save completion and return visits by making the benefit concrete. Measure signup → first save and seven-day return against the existing “Watchlist” copy; no lift is claimed until instrumentation exists.
