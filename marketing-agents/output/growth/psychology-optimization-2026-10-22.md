## Psychology Optimization — 2026-10-22

**Principle:** Endowment effect, reinforced by commitment and consistency.

**Where:** Post-signup empty saved-events state and the returning user dashboard.

**Exact copy/UX change:**

> Your shortlist is empty. Save your first event to keep its date, venue, onsale details, and official ticket links in one place.

Button: **Find an event to save**

Use “Your shortlist” and “My saved events” consistently so the user sees a personal collection taking shape. Do not mention price tracking, alerts, price movement, or urgency.

**Expected impact:** Hypothesis: ownership framing plus one concrete next action will increase first saved-event creation among new users. Measure signup → first saved event within 24 hours and guard against increased bounce rate.
