# Psychology optimization — 2026-10-23

## Principle

Endowment effect, reinforced by commitment and consistency.

## Touchpoint

The confirmation shown immediately after a user saves an event, plus the returning-user dashboard.

## Exact copy and UX change

> Saved to **your ticket list**: {{event_name}} on {{event_date}} at {{venue}}. Review onsale timing, venue tips, and buying links whenever you’re ready.

Primary CTA: **Review your saved event**. Secondary CTA: **Find another event**. Add the low-pressure note: “You can remove it anytime.”

## Expected impact

Hypothesis: a concrete saved item will increase first-save completion and return visits more effectively than an abstract “watchlist” confirmation. Measure signup → first save, first save → list review, and seven-day return once funnel events exist.

## Guardrail

Do not mention price drops, price history, alerts, price trends, or buy timing. The value proposition here is event discovery, onsale/presale information, venue guidance, and links to buy.
