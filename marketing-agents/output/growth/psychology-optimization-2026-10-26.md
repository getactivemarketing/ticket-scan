## Psychology optimization — 2026-10-26

**Principle:** Endowment effect, reinforced by commitment and consistency.

**Touchpoint:** first-save confirmation and returning-user dashboard.

**Copy:**

> Saved to **your ticket list**: {{event_name}} on {{event_date}} at {{venue}}. Review onsale timing, venue tips, and purchase links whenever you’re ready.

Primary CTA: **Review your saved event**. Secondary CTA: **Find another event**. Add: “You can remove it anytime.”

**Why this fits:** It makes the user’s saved event concrete and useful without creating false urgency or implying unavailable price functionality.

**Expected impact:** Hypothesis: higher first-save completion and more return visits. Instrument signup → first save, first save → list review, and seven-day return before judging the effect.
