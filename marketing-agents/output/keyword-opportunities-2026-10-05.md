## Competitor gap hypotheses

- SeatGeek’s visible editorial strength is named-tour presale coverage with sale-window tables and marketplace-specific tools such as interactive maps and Deal Score. Opportunity: build neutral checklists that remain useful across sellers and cover venue entry, delivery, and conditional-game details.
- StubHub’s recent coverage explains fees, buyer protection, and all-in pricing from the marketplace’s perspective. Opportunity: create an independent “compare the total and terms” framework that does not repeat one marketplace’s product pitch.
- Both competitors tend to lead with a specific artist or marketplace. TicketScan can own the intersection of event discovery, onsale/presale timing, and verified venue guides.
