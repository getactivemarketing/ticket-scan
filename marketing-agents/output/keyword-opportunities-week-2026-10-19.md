## SEO handoff rules

- Use official league, venue, artist, or seller sources for dates and onsale details.
- Add a last-checked date to time-sensitive sections.
- Do not invent availability, event counts, prices, or ticket inventory.
- If an event feed does not show a real event, omit the event-specific section rather than padding it.
- Link to TicketScan for event discovery, venue guides, and saved events—not price alerts or buy signals.
