# Feature Launch: Saved Event Starter Loop

## Promise

Help a new user find one event, save the relevant details, and understand the next action: review onsale timing, venue guidance, and outbound ticket links.

## Launch plan

- **Pre-launch:** instrument signup/search/save/saved-view/seller-click; build skippable one-screen onboarding; interview users with 2+ saved events.
- **Launch:** release to 25% of new accounts; email “Your ticket plan starts with one saved event”; publish a help article; share three social posts.
- **Post-launch:** compare signup-to-first-save and saved-page-to-seller-click against baseline; interview skips and completions; wait 30 days before claiming retention impact.

## Success criteria

Primary hypothesis: +20% relative signup-to-first-save improvement. Guardrails: under 60 seconds to complete, no increase in signup errors or unsubscribe complaints.
