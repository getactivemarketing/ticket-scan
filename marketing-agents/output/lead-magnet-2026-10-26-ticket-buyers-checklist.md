# Lead Magnet: The Ticket Buyer’s Checkout Checklist

## Asset spec

- **Format:** 3–4 page PDF, mobile-friendly, plus a one-page print version.
- **Audience:** People who have found an event and are deciding whether a listing is safe and understandable enough to buy.
- **Primary CTA:** “Find your event and venue guide at TicketScan.”
- **Secondary CTA:** “Join the TicketScan newsletter for event and onsale guidance.”
- **Capture fields:** Email address only; optional first name should remain optional.
- **Tone:** Calm, practical, slightly skeptical of rushed checkout screens.
- **Do not include:** TicketScan price history, price-drop alerts, target-price alerts, buy/wait calls, or TicketScan-derived price statistics.
- **Design notes:** Large checkboxes, high contrast, generous whitespace, room for handwritten budget and event details. Use a neutral ticket/venue illustration, not a fake price chart.

## Cover

### The Ticket Buyer’s Checkout Checklist

Know what you are buying before the countdown, queue, or seat map makes the decision for you.

Use this sheet for concerts, sports, theater, festivals, and major events.

## Page 1 — Before you open the seat map

Write down:

- Event: __________________________________________
- Date and start time: ______________________________
- Venue and city: __________________________________
- Maximum all-in budget: $__________________________
- Acceptable sections or view limits: ________________
- Number of tickets: ________________________________

### Confirm the event

- [ ] The artist/team/show name matches the official event.
- [ ] The date, start time, and venue are correct.
- [ ] The event is in the correct city and time zone.
- [ ] The seller or marketplace is the intended destination.
- [ ] The event page does not say postponed, canceled, or venue changed.

### Make a simple plan

Decide before the queue opens:

- Your maximum all-in total.
- Your first-choice sections.
- Two backup sections.
- Whether you will accept obstructed, limited, or partial views.
- Whether mobile transfer or delayed delivery works for your plans.

## Page 2 — Read the listing, not just the headline number

### Seat and section check

- [ ] Section, row, and seat numbers are shown or clearly described.
- [ ] The venue map matches the event configuration.
- [ ] “Limited view,” “obstructed view,” “behind stage,” or similar notes are understood.
- [ ] The seats are together if you need adjacent seats.
- [ ] The listing is for a ticket, not a parking pass, hospitality package, suite, or add-on.
- [ ] The entry gate or venue level is practical for your group.

### Listing and delivery check

- [ ] The listing is clearly labeled as primary, resale, transfer, or another category.
- [ ] Delivery method is stated.
- [ ] Delivery timing works before the event.
- [ ] Account, app, ID, or transfer requirements are understood.
- [ ] Refund, cancellation, replacement, and delayed-delivery terms are visible.

## Page 3 — Expand the checkout total

Do not decide from the first number on the ticket card.

- [ ] Ticket quantity is correct.
- [ ] Base ticket total is recorded.
- [ ] Service fees are visible.
- [ ] Delivery or transfer fees are visible.
- [ ] Taxes and venue fees are visible.
- [ ] Any membership, exchange, or payment-condition requirement is understood.
- [ ] Final total is within the budget written on page 1.
- [ ] Currency is correct.

### Compare fairly

If you are checking more than one seller, compare the same event, date, section, seat quality, delivery terms, and final checkout total. A lower headline number is not automatically a lower total or a better ticket.

## Page 4 — Final safety check

- [ ] The web address is the seller’s real domain.
- [ ] You are not paying by an irreversible method requested outside the platform.
- [ ] The seller’s buyer-protection terms are readable.
- [ ] You have not been pushed into a purchase by a countdown you cannot verify.
- [ ] You saved the order number, confirmation email, and delivery instructions.
- [ ] You know where to find support if the ticket does not arrive.

### If something feels wrong

Pause. Recheck the event page, seller, section, delivery terms, and final total. It is fine to leave a seat map and come back after you understand the listing.
