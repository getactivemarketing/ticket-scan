## The three callouts (for the footer + email sequence)

**1. The get-in spread.** Three cheapest floors: Levi's $80 / Gillette $110 / Lumen $110. Three priciest: NRG $135 / MetLife $150 / SoFi $150. That's roughly a **45% premium** to get into the marquee-venue cluster versus the bargain cluster — same Cat-4 tier, same sport. The pattern isn't random: the cheapest floors sit at venues hosting **zero or few knockout matches.** When a building hosts the Final, a Semifinal, or Quarterfinals, FIFA's variable-pricing engine lifts the *entire* ladder, including the cheap seats. Prestige is contagious, and it's expensive.

**2. The $80 seat isn't actually the cheapest.** Levi's lists the lowest ticket in the tournament — and the **worst transit in the portfolio** (a VTA transfer most fans skip for paid parking). Add two rideshares at $35–$50 and that $80 seat is ~$160 all-in for a solo traveler. **Lincoln Financial Field ($125 floor) is arguably the cheapest *all-in* World Cup ticket in America** once you count getting there: SEPTA's Broad Street Line runs Center City → stadium for **$2.50 each way**, ~15 minutes. Lumen (downtown Seattle) and NRG (Houston METRORail, $1.25) are the other genuinely transit-friendly venues.

**3. The roof column can outrank the price column.** Several US host cities run 90°F-plus with afternoon thunderstorms in June/July. Three venues retract (AT&T, Mercedes-Benz, NRG) and Mercedes-Benz pairs that with the cheapest concessions in the tournament ($2 hot dogs, $5 beer). SoFi and Hard Rock have fixed canopies; the other six are fully open-air. If you're heat-sensitive, a covered seat in Houston or Atlanta is a comfort upgrade you can't buy at an open-air bowl at any price.

**Footer synthesis (one paragraph):** *There's no single "best value" US World Cup stadium — there's the one that matches your constraint. Optimizing for the cheapest ticket points you at Levi's or a $110 venue (budget for the car). Cheapest all-in trip points you at Lincoln Financial, Lumen, or NRG, where transit is nearly free. Best comfort in a hot host city points you at the retractable-roof venues. Pick your constraint, then use this map. And before you check out, compare the same seat across Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, and StubHub at ticketscan.io — the gap between platforms is often bigger than the gap between venues.*
