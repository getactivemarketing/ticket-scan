## 1. Check the all-in total

Write down the number you will actually pay, not the first number in the card.

- [ ] Fees are shown before payment.
- [ ] Taxes and delivery are included.
- [ ] The total is for the exact number of tickets I need.
- [ ] I compared the same section or an equivalent seat, not a headline price from a different location.

**Quick rule:** if two listings are not for comparable seats, their totals do not answer the same question.

## 2. Check the seat, not just the section name

“Lower level,” “club,” and “floor” can mean different things at different venues.

- [ ] I opened the venue’s current seating map.
- [ ] I checked the row, view, and any obstructed-view note.
- [ ] I know whether the listing is standing room, general admission, or reserved seating.
- [ ] I checked the venue guide for entry gates and section-specific notes.

## 3. Check who is selling the ticket

Before paying, identify whether the listing is from the primary seller, an authorized resale marketplace, or an individual resale listing.

- [ ] The seller or marketplace is clearly named.
- [ ] The listing says when and how the ticket will be delivered.
- [ ] The buyer guarantee or refund policy is visible.
- [ ] I know what happens if the event is canceled, postponed, or the ticket is invalid.

## 4. Check the delivery and entry rules

Many ticket problems happen after checkout.

- [ ] I know whether the ticket is mobile-only.
- [ ] I know whether transfer is allowed.
- [ ] The account or app I need is ready before event day.
- [ ] I saved the venue address, gate information, and a backup way to access the ticket.

## 5. Check the event details one last time

Event listings can change. Confirm the basics immediately before paying.

- [ ] Date and local start time are correct.
- [ ] Venue and city are correct.
- [ ] The event is the performance or game I actually want.
- [ ] Any presale code, membership, or purchase limit is understood.

## Red flags: pause before paying

- The listing omits the total until the final step.
- The seat map does not match the listing description.
- A seller asks you to pay outside the marketplace.
- The event date, venue, or transfer terms are unclear.
- The offer pressures you to skip the buyer-protection terms.
