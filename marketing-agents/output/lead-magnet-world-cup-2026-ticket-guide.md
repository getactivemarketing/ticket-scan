## Page 5 — Where to Buy: Platform Comparison

### The 4 main channels for World Cup 2026 tickets

#### 1. FIFA Official (fifa.com/tickets)
- **Pros:** Face value, guaranteed authentic
- **Cons:** Limited inventory, dynamic pricing can spike above resale, lottery-based for high-demand matches
- **Best for:** Group stage games at face value

#### 2. StubHub
- **Pros:** Large inventory, buyer guarantee, mobile delivery
- **Cons:** Fees add 20-30% to listed price
- **Best for:** Knockout round tickets, last-minute purchases

#### 3. SeatGeek
- **Pros:** "Deal Score" rating, transparent all-in pricing
- **Cons:** Smaller World Cup inventory than StubHub
- **Best for:** Finding deals on less popular group matches

#### 4. Ticketmaster (Resale)
- **Pros:** Official resale partner for many venues
- **Cons:** Fees can be opaque, inventory varies
- **Best for:** Venue-specific listings

## Page 7 — Money-Saving Tips

### 10 ways to save on World Cup 2026 tickets

1. **Compare across platforms, every time.** The same ticket can vary by $50-$187 depending on where you buy. Use TicketScan to check all sources at once.

2. **Target group stage games at smaller venues.** BMO Field (30K capacity) and Estadio Akron (50K) will have lower demand than MetLife (82K for the Final).

3. **Go to games without the USA or Mexico.** Host nation matches carry a massive premium. A Serbia vs. Morocco group game will be a fraction of the price.

4. **Buy in pairs, not singles.** Sellers often list pairs at a slight per-ticket discount. Four singles will almost always cost more than two pairs.

5. **Set price alerts and be patient.** TicketScan tracks price history. Many tickets will have a lower price point before the tournament — our alerts catch those dips.

6. **Watch for FIFA's official resale opening.** FIFA opens an official resale marketplace closer to the event. Prices are capped closer to face value.

7. **Avoid "VIP packages" unless you specifically want hospitality.** Package deals bundle tickets with overpriced extras. If you just want to watch the game, buy tickets separately.

8. **Check all-in pricing.** StubHub's listed price doesn't include fees. SeatGeek shows all-in pricing. Always compare the final checkout price, not the listed price.

9. **Consider travel packages carefully.** Some offer good value. Many are overpriced bundles. Price the components (flight + hotel + ticket) separately first.

10. **Don't wait until matchups are confirmed for knockout rounds.** Prices spike once specific teams are placed. If you know you want to attend a quarter-final at Hard Rock Stadium regardless of teams, buy early.
