# Paywall & Upgrade Flow — 2026-10-09

## Decision

No paywall this week. Keep event discovery, saved events, and venue-guide access free while the current product does not have a reliable premium capability to gate.

## Future touchpoints

| Trigger | UX | Copy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Verified free limit reached | Inline banner | Explain the concrete live benefit and show terms clearly |
| User opens a live premium feature | Feature gate | Offer a preview, monthly/annual price, cancellation, and restore path |
| User has completed onboarding and returned twice | Non-blocking card | Invite support for convenience or organization, not unavailable tracking outcomes |

Do not gate the first saved event. When billing is eventually tested, include annual savings, renewal terms, cancellation, refund policy, and restore-purchase behavior. Never use a price-alert or prediction promise until the underlying feature is verified live.
