# Paywall & Upgrade Flow — Recommendation

## Decision

No production paywall this week. Do not gate saved events, venue guides, event discovery, onsale timing, or seller links. Do not show upgrade prompts for unavailable price features.

## Future touchpoints

| Trigger | UX | Copy direction |
|---|---|---|
| Verified free limit reached | Inline banner | Explain one working added capability; let the current task finish |
| Live premium feature opened | Contextual gate | Show the feature, price, free preview, and cancellation terms |
| User has completed onboarding and returned twice | Non-blocking card | Offer organization/convenience, not unavailable tracking outcomes |

## Required checkout information

Show monthly and annual plans, annual savings, renewal terms, cancellation path, refund policy, support contact, and restore-purchase action. Never delete or hide saved data when a user declines.

## Future copy template

“Keep your ticket planning organized with [available feature]. Choose monthly or annual access. You can cancel anytime.”

Do not implement until the feature is real, instrumented, supportable, and reliable for 30 days.
