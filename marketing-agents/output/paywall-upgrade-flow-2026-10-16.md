# Paywall / Upgrade Flow Spec

## Current decision

No production paywall. Do not gate the currently useful free workflow or present unavailable price features as paid benefits.

## Future touchpoints

- Contextual education card for a real, measurable paid feature.
- Soft limit after the current task is complete; never delete saved data.
- Settings upgrade page with monthly/annual pricing, terms, cancellation, and support.
- Post-value prompt only after a successful measurable action.

**Copy template:** “Keep your ticket planning organized with [available feature]. Choose monthly or annual access. You can cancel anytime.”
