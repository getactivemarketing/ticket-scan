# Pricing & Monetization Recommendation — 2026-10-09

## Recommendation: affiliate-first, premium later

TicketScan should remain free for now. First instrument `seller_outbound_click` with event, page, partner, placement, and campaign metadata, then reconcile clicks and commissions weekly. Continue the CJ/TicketNetwork product-feed work and evaluate Stay22 and Viator for venue-guide monetization.

Advertising can wait until page speed, inventory quality, and affiliate economics are understood. A paid tier is premature: the currently proposed history, alert, and prediction benefits are not reliable product value today.

## Future premium test

Keep $7.99/month or $59/year as a hypothesis only. Revisit after a durable feature has billing, entitlements, cancellation, refund handling, delivery telemetry, and 30 days of reliable operation. The first paid value should be a working convenience or organization feature, not an unavailable tracking promise.

## Immediate success measures

- Outbound click rate by placement and event page.
- Click-to-partner landing success where available.
- Affiliate earnings per 1,000 sessions.
- Referral signup and saved-event conversion.
- Activation and 30-day return after instrumentation exists.
