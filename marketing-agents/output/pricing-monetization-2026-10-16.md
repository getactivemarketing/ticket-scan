# TicketScan Pricing & Monetization Recommendation

## Decision

Use an affiliate-first model now; defer subscriptions and keep advertising selective.

## Sequence

1. Repair/implement TicketNetwork CJ feed and outbound-click attribution.
2. Measure seller-link impressions, clicks, destination, event, and commission reconciliation.
3. Keep discovery, saved events, venue guides, onsale timing, and outbound links free.
4. Test a future $5–8/month or $49–69/year tier only for validated, available discovery/organization tools.

Do not sell price history, price alerts, price predictions, or buy-timing calls while those capabilities are unavailable.
