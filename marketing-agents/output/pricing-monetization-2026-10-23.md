# Pricing & Monetization Recommendation — 2026-10-23

## Decision

Affiliate-first now, premium later, advertising last.

## Sequence

1. Implement TicketNetwork/CJ feed work and seller-link attribution: impression, click, seller, event, placement, session, and commission reconciliation.
2. Keep event discovery, saved events, venue guides, onsale/presale information, and outbound links free.
3. Test a future $5–8/month or $49–69/year tier only for validated discovery/organization tools such as advanced filters, saved-list organization, exports, or early guide access.
4. Test clearly labeled, non-intrusive ads only after page speed and affiliate conversion are understood.

Do not sell price history, price alerts, predictions, or buy-timing calls while those capabilities are unavailable. Do not launch a checkout flow until entitlements, cancellation, refunds, and support are implemented and a real paid feature has passed a 30-day reliability window.

## Gate to revisit premium

- Seven-day conversion telemetry integrity window passes.
- At least one durable feature has repeated usage evidence.
- Billing and entitlement QA passes.
- Users can see pricing, renewal, cancellation, refund, and restore-purchase terms before payment.
