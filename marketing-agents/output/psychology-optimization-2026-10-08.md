## Psychology Optimization — 2026-10-08

**Principle:** Endowment effect / ownership framing.

**Where:** Post-signup empty-watchlist state and the returning user’s dashboard.

**Exact copy/UX change:** Replace a generic “Add to watchlist” prompt with:

> Your shortlist is empty. Save your first event to keep its onsale details, venue guide, and purchase links in one place.
