# Psychology Optimization — 2026-10-24

**Principle:** endowment effect, reinforced by commitment and consistency.

**Touchpoint:** event cards after search and the empty saved-events state.

**Exact copy:**

> Save this event so it’s ready when you are. We’ll keep the date, venue, onsale details, venue guide, and official ticket links together in your saved events.

Button: **Save this event**

Empty state: **Your first saved event is one search away — try a team, artist, venue, or city.**

**Expected impact:** make ownership tangible and give a newly registered user one obvious next step. Measure signup-to-first-saved-event conversion, first-session saves, and 7-day return visits. No uplift estimate is justified until instrumentation exists.
