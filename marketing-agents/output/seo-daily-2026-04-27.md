## Competitor Insight

Spot-checked **Vivid Seats** (`https://www.vividseats.com/`) — the one major U.S. ticket comparison surface not yet sampled in the rotation. Findings:

- **Vivid Seats emits two separate JSON-LD blocks per route** — one for `Organization` + `WebSite` (consolidated, identical across routes), one for the per-page entity (`Event`, `BreadcrumbList`, etc.). Same architectural pattern as SeatGeek/StubHub (per 2026-04-26 spot-check). Three-of-three competitors confirm: canonical Org + WebSite from a global root layout, page-level entities cross-reference via `@id`.
- **Differentiator:** Vivid Seats' `Event` entity carries `eventStatus: "EventScheduled"` and `eventAttendanceMode: "OfflineEventAttendanceMode"` — two fields neither SeatGeek nor StubHub emit, and which our `/tickets/[slug]` schema also lacks. **Both fields are Google rich-result eligibility requirements for `Event` schema as of the 2024 spec update.** Adding them to the `AggregateOffer`/`Event` block in `web/src/app/tickets/[slug]/page.tsx` is a ~4-line change. **Logging only — not adding to the bundle.** Queue as a post-bundle item; a 13-file bundle is not improved by becoming a 14-file bundle.
- All three competitors render their global Org+WebSite block server-side from a top-level template/layout — confirming for the third day running that yesterday's "WebSite-from-root-layout" relocation is the right convergence target. We've now exhausted the competitor-research surface for global JSON-LD architecture; further competitor checks on this question would be redundant.
