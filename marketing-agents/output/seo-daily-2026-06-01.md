## 2. Schema Markup Validation — price-bearing templates (today's rotation)

Focused on the two templates where schema↔display price reconciliation matters
most, since recent SEO requests flagged WC pricing-schema issues
(`wc-lowprice-erratum`, `wc-category-pricing-canonical`).

### 🔴 DEFECT FOUND & FIXED — `world-cup-2026/[stadium]/page.tsx`

**Bug 1 — `lowPrice` reported the *most expensive* tier as the get-in price.**
`extractLowestPrice()` iterated sections **backward and returned the first
match** — i.e. the *last* section in the array. But the **Suite Level is the
last section in every US stadium's `sections` array**, and it is always the
single most expensive tier. So the `AggregateOffer.lowPrice` in the JSON-LD on
all 11 US World Cup pages advertised the *suite* price as the cheapest ticket.

Runtime simulation against the real `worldcup.ts` data (old logic vs. fixed):

| Stadium | Old `lowPrice` (wrong) | New `lowPrice` (true floor) |
|---|---|---|
| MetLife | $15,000 | **$150** |
| SoFi | $22,000 | **$150** |
| Hard Rock | $12,000 | **$120** |
| AT&T | $15,000 | **$130** |
| Mercedes-Benz | $10,000 | **$120** |
| Lumen Field | $8,000 | **$110** |
| Levi's | $6,000 | **$80** |
| Gillette | $5,000 | **$110** |
| Lincoln Financial | $5,500 | **$125** |
| NRG | $7,000 | **$135** |
| Arrowhead | $6,500 | **$110** |

That is a ~40–275× overstatement of the get-in price, and it directly
contradicted the on-page "Cheapest World Cup seats … $80–$150" sections — exactly
the kind of schema↔content mismatch Google flags as a structured-data quality
issue, with a real risk of an absurd "from $15,000" rich result going live days
before the tournament.

**Fix:** rewrote `extractLowestPrice()` to scan **all** sections for the true
minimum, removing the fragile "last section" ordering assumption.

**Bug 2 — currency mismatch on the 5 non-US venues.** `priceCurrency` was
hardcoded `'USD'`, but BMO Field / BC Place quote **CAD** and the three Mexican
stadiums quote **MXN** (e.g. Estadio Azteca `$150 MXN`). The schema declared
those local-currency figures as USD.

**Fix:** derive `priceCurrency` from `venue.country` (USA→USD, Canada→CAD,
Mexico→MXN). lowPrice magnitudes for those 5 venues were already correct (their
last section happens to be the cheapest); only the currency label was wrong.

**Verification:** `tsc --noEmit` clean on the changed file; old-vs-new values
simulated against live data (table above). No other code paths touched.
