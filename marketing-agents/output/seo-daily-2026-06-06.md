## 5. Competitor / enhancement insight (Task 3, in lieu of a fix)

**Venue pages render live events but don't expose them in schema.** Major resale
competitors (SeatGeek, StubHub) emit an `ItemList` of `Event` nodes (each with
`name`, `startDate`, `location`, and an `offers`/`AggregateOffer` price) on their
venue pages — which is what earns the event-list rich treatment for "[venue]
tickets" queries. Our `venues/[slug]` page already fetches `getVenueEvents()` and
renders them visually (:187–225) but the JSON-LD only describes the `Place`.

- **Opportunity:** mirror the already-fetched events into an `ItemList` /
  `Event` graph on the venue page (low risk — data is already in hand; no new API).
- **Deliberately NOT applied today** to avoid enlarging the undeployed diff before
  it ships. Re-evaluate as the *first* post-deploy enhancement.
