existing working-tree bundle**, not adding to it — every new uncommitted edit only enlarges an

**Note on `logo.png`:** it is the single asset behind *every* JSON-LD `logo`/`publisher.logo`
ref **and** every blog `Article.image` site-wide, yet it is git-untracked while live-200. A
clean-checkout redeploy would 404 it and silently break image/logo schema everywhere. `git add
web/public/logo.png` is a one-file, low-risk track that should ride along with the deploy commit.

**Competitor insight (spot-check, in lieu of a fix):** SeatGeek/StubHub blog posts ship a real
hero photo as the `Article` `image` (not their logo), so their posts are eligible for the article
thumbnail in SERPs and Discover. Our posts currently can't compete for that thumbnail. Closing
this is the cheapest blog-SERP-CTR win once images exist — see Content ask below.
