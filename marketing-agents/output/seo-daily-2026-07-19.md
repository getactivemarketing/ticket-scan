## Technical Fix Applied Today

### Fix: Removed expiring "before kickoff" language from WC main page meta
**File:** `web/src/app/world-cup-2026/page.tsx`

**Why:** The previous meta description ended with "find the best deal before kickoff" — language that becomes inaccurate the moment the Final whistle blows. Since Google re-crawls high-traffic pages within hours and the post-match search spike ("World Cup Final score", "who won World Cup 2026", "World Cup Final tickets resale") will pull organic traffic to this page today, stale pre-game framing reduces CTR on post-match queries.

**Before:**
```
description: 'The World Cup 2026 Final is July 19 at MetLife Stadium. Compare ticket prices across Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, and StubHub. Set a price alert and find the best deal before kickoff.'
```

**After:**
```
description: 'World Cup 2026 Final — July 19 at MetLife Stadium. Compare ticket prices across Ticketmaster, SeatGeek, and StubHub to see which platform has the best deal.'
```

OG and Twitter descriptions updated to match (removed "before kickoff" / "set an alert" framing, replaced with neutral present-tense). All three now read correctly for both pre-match searchers and post-match retrospective traffic.

No sitemap bump needed — `CONTENT_LAST_MODIFIED` should be bumped only after the post-Final blog posts are published (target: July 20–21).
