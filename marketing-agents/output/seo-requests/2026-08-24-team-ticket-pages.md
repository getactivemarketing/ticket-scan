# Content request: team-specific ticket landing pages

## Opportunity

Create crawlable landing pages for high-intent team queries that are not covered cleanly by the current city/category namespace:

- Orlando Magic tickets
- New York Knicks tickets
- Los Angeles Lakers tickets
- New York Rangers tickets

## Suggested page requirements

- Explain how TicketScan compares prices and fees across marketplaces.
- Include current upcoming events only, with event date, venue, and lowest available price when present.
- Link to the relevant city page, venue guide, compare flow, and price-alert signup.
- Add unique title, meta description, `SportsTeam`/`ItemList` schema where appropriate, and sitemap entries.
- Avoid publishing stale prices or implying TicketScan sells tickets directly.

## SEO rationale

SeatGeek’s live navigation exposes team-level pages at scale. Team pages would strengthen internal linking between TicketScan’s existing venue/city/category content and capture searches such as “cheap Knicks tickets” and “Orlando Magic tickets price comparison.”
