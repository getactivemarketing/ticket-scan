# SEO request — historical World Cup ticket pricing hub

## Opportunity

Create a factual, post-event landing page targeting:

- World Cup 2026 ticket prices
- World Cup Final 2026 ticket cost
- World Cup 2026 ticket price history

## Guidance

- State clearly that the tournament concluded on July 19, 2026.
- Separate advertised resale ranges, TicketScan estimates, and verified transaction data.
- Link to the existing World Cup hub and 16 stadium pages.
- Include a methodology section explaining dates, currencies, fees, and platform coverage.
- Add `Article` or `Dataset` schema only if the page contains a durable dataset with a clear publisher and update date.

## Source

SEO daily audit for 2026-08-25. The opportunity is based on the site’s existing World Cup content and current post-event search intent; Search Console query volume was not available.
