# SEO opportunities — 2026-10-04

Based on a competitor spot-check, consider content briefs for:

- How ticket presales work: artist, venue, cardholder, and fan-club access.
- Stadium seating guides: section naming, sightlines, and how to read a seating chart.
- Accessible ticket buying: ADA inventory, companion seating, and what to verify on the seller page.
- All-in ticket checkout: fees, delivery, taxes, and speculative-listing warnings.

Each brief should use venue-specific facts from the verified data files and should link to the relevant venue, city, and onsale pages. Do not promise TicketScan price history, price alerts, or buy/wait recommendations while those systems are down.
