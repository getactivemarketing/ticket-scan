# SEO requests — 2026-10-16

## Safe internal-linking and content opportunities

SeatGeek's live NFL hub uses a clear event/category/venue link structure and dedicated subtopics for cheap and last-minute discovery. TicketScan can pursue the same information architecture without making unavailable price-tracking claims:

- Link each venue guide to its city page, relevant team pages, onsale calendar, and two verified venue/access articles.
- Link city/category pages to the matching venue guides and the onsale calendar.
- Prioritize articles around seating sections, capacity/access, onsale and presale timing, all-in fees, primary versus resale listings, and speculative listings.
- Do not add TicketScan claims about price history, price trends, alerts, target prices, or buy/wait recommendations.

Suggested safe targets:

- `[venue] seating sections and capacity`
- `[venue] event schedule`
- `when do [event] tickets go on sale`
- `[city] concert tickets onsale calendar`
- `how to compare ticket fees`
- `primary vs resale tickets`
- `how to read a ticket seat map`
