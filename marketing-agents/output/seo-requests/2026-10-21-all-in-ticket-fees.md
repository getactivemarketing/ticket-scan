# SEO request: all-in ticket fees guide

## Opportunity

Create an evergreen guide targeting:

- all-in ticket prices
- ticket fees explained
- how to compare ticket prices with fees
- ticket checkout total vs ticket price

## Why now

SeatGeek's buyer documentation explicitly explains that displayed prices include mandatory fees, while StubHub's support documentation explains fee-inclusive browsing. This is a durable consumer question and fits TicketScan's current, truthful advice: follow the seller link and confirm the final checkout total.

## Suggested angle

“The Ticket Price Is Not the Total: How to Compare All-In Costs”

Cover service fees, delivery fees, taxes, optional add-ons, identical event/date/section checks, and why the seller checkout page is the source of truth. Link to `/faq`, `/how-it-works`, relevant venue guides, and event discovery pages.

## Guardrails

Do not say TicketScan tracks prices, maintains price history, sends price-drop alerts, or produces buy/wait/hold recommendations. Do not quote TicketScan price statistics. Attribute current fee behavior to each seller's own documentation and add a “fees change” disclaimer.
