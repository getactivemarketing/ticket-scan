## Publishing guardrails

- Do not claim TicketScan tracks prices, stores price history, sends price
  alerts, or gives buy/wait/hold recommendations.
- Do not use TicketScan-derived price statistics or “our data shows” language.
- Do not call a saved event a price-tracking item.
- Do not publish a named matchup, venue, or onsale time without a source and
  last-checked timestamp.
- Do not present World Cup 2026 as upcoming or active until the product owner
  resolves the status/date conflict.
- Do not publish to TikTok; TicketScan has no active TikTok account.
- Do not mark a post live without a platform post ID or public URL.
