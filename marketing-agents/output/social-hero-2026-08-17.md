## Slide copy and design spec

### Slide 1 — Cover

**On-image copy:** 5 ways to stop overpaying for tickets  
**Support line:** The headline price is only the opening argument.  
**Visual:** A ticket-price card with the total partially hidden behind a “+ fees” tab. Small TicketScan mark in the corner.

### Slide 2 — Set the ceiling first

**On-image copy:** 1. Pick your max before you browse  
**Body:** Write down the most you are comfortable paying—including fees and delivery. If you decide after the countdown starts, the countdown is negotiating for you.  
**Visual:** Price ceiling line above a scrolling listing stack.

### Slide 3 — Match the event

**On-image copy:** 2. Same event or totally different deal?  
**Body:** Match artist/team, date, venue, session, and ticket quantity before comparing numbers.  
**Visual:** Two event cards with four matching checkmarks.

### Slide 4 — Match the seat

**On-image copy:** 3. Section is not the whole seat  
**Body:** Check section, row quality, view, quantity, accessibility notes, and delivery type. A cheaper ticket is not cheaper if the inventory is different.  
**Visual:** Simplified seat map with two equivalent seats highlighted.

### Slide 5 — Check the total

**On-image copy:** 4. Compare the number you will actually pay  
**Body:** Illustrative example: $120 listing + $36 fees = $156 checkout total. Label examples as examples; verify live totals at checkout.  
**Visual:** Calculator-style transformation: `$120` → `+$36` → `$156`.

### Slide 6 — Add a timestamp

**On-image copy:** 5. A price without a timestamp is just a number  
**Body:** Record when you checked. Inventory, fees, and prices move. Stale data is context—not a live buy signal.  
**Visual:** Timestamp badge beside a price card; use “checked at” rather than a fake live marketplace state.

### Slide 7 — Know when to walk

**On-image copy:** Your ceiling is allowed to say no  
**Body:** “Low inventory” is not a budget. If the final total crosses your limit, change the date, section, or event—or walk away.  
**Visual:** Red ceiling line and a clean exit arrow; slightly irreverent tone.

### Slide 8 — CTA

**On-image copy:** Compare first. Buy with context.  
**Body:** TicketScan helps you compare available sources with the same event and equivalent inventory in view.  
**CTA:** Start at `ticketscan.io/compare`  
**Visual:** Four small labels: event / seat / total / time.
