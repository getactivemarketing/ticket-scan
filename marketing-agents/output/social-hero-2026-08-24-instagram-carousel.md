## Slide copy

### Slide 1 — Cover

**5 ways to stop overpaying for tickets**

The ticket price is not the whole story.

Small text: A five-minute buyer check from TicketScan.

### Slide 2 — Set your ceiling first

**1. Pick your all-in maximum before you browse.**

Write down the most you will pay including fees and delivery. Otherwise, the first listing becomes your budget by accident.

### Slide 3 — Compare equivalent tickets

**2. Match the event, date, section, quantity, and delivery.**

A lower number is not a deal if it buys a different seat, fewer tickets, or slower delivery.

### Slide 4 — Check the final total

**3. Judge the number your card sees.**

Listing price is the opening argument. The checkout total gets the final vote.

### Slide 5 — Give the price a timestamp

**4. Record when you checked.**

Ticket prices move. A number without a timestamp is context-free—and not a reason to panic-buy.

### Slide 6 — Choose your tradeoff

**5. Decide what you will trade: money, time, or seat choice.**

Waiting can create options, but inventory can thin. Buying early can reduce stress, but certainty has a price. Make the tradeoff on purpose.

### Slide 7 — CTA

**Compare before checkout.**

Search the same event across available sources, check the total, then save the event you may actually buy.

**Start at ticketscan.io/compare**

Small text: Prices, inventory, fees, delivery terms, and event details can change. Verify final checkout terms with the seller.
