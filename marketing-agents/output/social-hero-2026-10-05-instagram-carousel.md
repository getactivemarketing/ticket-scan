## Slide copy

### Slide 1 — Hook

**The presale code is not the whole plan.**

Access gets you into the window. It does not promise a particular seat.

### Slide 2 — Confirm the exact event

Before the queue opens, check:

- Artist or team
- City and venue
- Exact date and start time
- Whether the event is guaranteed or conditional

### Slide 3 — Put the sale time in your time zone

Write down:

- Sale type
- Date
- Time
- Time zone
- Ticket limit

“10 AM” is not a complete instruction until you know **10 AM where**.

### Slide 4 — Know what gets you access

Artist or fan-club presale? Venue or promoter presale? Cardholder or partner access?

Read the official rules. A code, card, or registration can open a window without guaranteeing inventory.

### Slide 5 — Compare the ticket, not just the number

Check the same fields every time:

- Section and row
- Number of seats together
- View notes
- Primary or resale
- Delivery timing

Different ticket ≠ useful comparison.

### Slide 6 — Read the final order summary

Before you approve payment, review:

- Full quantity
- Fees and taxes
- Delivery charges
- Transfer terms
- Buyer protection or seller terms

The checkout summary is the number that matters.

### Slide 7 — Calm beats panic

Set your rules before the queue starts:

- Maximum total spend
- Minimum acceptable view
- Number of seats
- Backup option

Find the event. Verify the window. Check the details.

**Save this for the next onsale.**
