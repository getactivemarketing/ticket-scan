## Slide copy

### Slide 1 — Hook

**Before you pay for a ticket, check these 7 things.**

Small text: The first listing is not the whole decision.

### Slide 2 — 1. The exact event

Right team or artist. Right opponent or show. Right game number. Right date.

Small text: “Friday night” is not an event identity.

### Slide 3 — 2. The venue and local time

Confirm the building and the venue’s local start time.

Small text: Presale and onsale times are usually local to the venue.

### Slide 4 — 3. The section and view

Open the seat map. Read limited-view, obstructed-view, standing-room, and stage-configuration notes.

Small text: A section label is a starting point, not a sightline.

### Slide 5 — 4. The ticket type

Standard ticket? Resale ticket? VIP package? Parking pass? Club access?

Small text: Similar names can mean very different entry rights.

### Slide 6 — 5. Delivery and seller terms

Check mobile transfer, delivery timing, seller identity, transfer restrictions, and refund rules.

Small text: “Instant” is a promise worth reading carefully.

### Slide 7 — 6. Quantity and comparability

Compare the same number of tickets and genuinely similar sections.

Small text: Two listings are not comparable just because they share a team name.

### Slide 8 — 7. The final checkout total

Expand fees, taxes, delivery, and buyer-protection terms before approval.

Small text: The first number is not the final bill.

### Slide 9 — CTA

**Save this checklist for your next game or show.**

Find the event, venue guide, and places to continue at TicketScan.
