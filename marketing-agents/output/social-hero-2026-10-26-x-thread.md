## Thread copy

### 1/8

The ticket listing is not the whole ticket.

Before you click through, check these six fields. Future-you, standing outside
the venue with a group chat melting down, will be grateful.

### 2/8

**1. Exact event**

Right team or artist. Right opponent or show. Right date. Right game number.

“Friday night” is not an event identity.

### 3/8

**2. Venue and local time**

Confirm the building and the venue’s local start time. A sale window or event
time can look different after your phone converts it to your time zone.

### 4/8

**3. Section and view**

Open the venue map. Read limited-view, obstructed-view, standing-room, and
stage-configuration notes.

A section label is a starting point, not a sightline.

### 5/8

**4. Ticket type**

Standard ticket? Resale ticket? VIP package? Parking pass? Club access?

Similar-looking listings can grant very different entry rights.

### 6/8

**5. Delivery and seller terms**

Check mobile transfer, delivery timing, seller identity, transfer restrictions,
refund language, and buyer-protection terms.

“Instant” is a promise worth reading carefully.

### 7/8

**6. Quantity and final total**

Compare the same number of tickets and genuinely similar sections. Then expand
fees, taxes, delivery, and protection terms before approval.

The first number is not the final bill.

### 8/8

Save this checklist for your next game or show:

**event · venue/time · section/view · ticket type · delivery/terms · quantity ·
final total**

Find the event and venue guidance at ticketscan.io.
