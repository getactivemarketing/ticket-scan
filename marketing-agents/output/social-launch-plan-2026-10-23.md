# Social Launch Plan — Saved Event Starter Loop

## Message

TicketScan helps people find an event, check the practical details, save it for later, and continue to the seller when they are ready.

## One-week plan

- **Mon / X:** “Found an event? Save the date and venue details so you can find them again.” CTA: Find your event.
- **Tue / Instagram:** Carousel: search → event details → venue guide → saved event. Use a real venue with verified section facts.
- **Wed / Threads:** Checklist: date, venue, onsale/presale timing, section, all-in total, delivery, seller terms.
- **Thu / X:** Referral beta prompt: “Share the event you’re planning with a friend.” CTA: Share event.
- **Fri / Instagram:** Venue-guide spotlight focused on named sections, capacity, access, and practical tips.

Use `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` consistently. Do not publish until outbound-click and referral attribution events are available for QA.

## Prohibited claims

No price tracking, price history, price-drop alerts, target-price alerts, buy/wait/hold calls, “our data shows,” savings statistics, or implication that TicketScan provides seller checkout or buyer protection.

## Success metrics

After instrumentation: link landing sessions, referral signups, first saves, seller outbound clicks, and qualified referrals. Until then, report posts and clicks only if the platform provides verifiable numbers; do not infer conversion.
