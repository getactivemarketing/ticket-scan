# Social → Paid Ads handoff — October 5, 2026

## Decision

No paid boost is recommended yet. There is no authenticated platform performance export, no confirmed post-level analytics, and no reliable conversion attribution. The queue contains drafts and scheduled records, not measured winners.

## Test candidates after measurement is connected

1. **Instagram hero carousel:** “The presale code is not the whole plan.” Optimize first for saves/shares, then test outbound clicks.
2. **Ticket Buyer’s Cheat Sheet:** optimize for qualified newsletter signups and guide downloads.
3. **Presale guide:** optimize for outbound clicks to the event/guide page and completed watchlist adds, with watchlist described only as saving an event for later review.

## Required evidence before spend

- Confirmed public post URL or platform post ID.
- At least seven days of organic results.
- Impressions/reach, engagement rate, saves/shares, link clicks, and UTM-attributed signup or download events.
- Creative review confirming no disabled price-tracking or alert claims.

## Copy guardrail
