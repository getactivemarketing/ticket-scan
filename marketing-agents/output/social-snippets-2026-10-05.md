## 2. Presale guide — Instagram

The presale code is not the whole plan.

Before tickets open:

1. Confirm the exact date and venue.
2. Check the time zone and ticket limit.
3. Use the official artist, venue, promoter, or ticketing link.
4. Compare the seat, delivery terms, and full checkout total.

Access gets you into the shopping window. It does not promise a particular seat.
