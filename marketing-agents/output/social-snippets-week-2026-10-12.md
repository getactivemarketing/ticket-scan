## Presale explainer

Presale does not always mean “better seat.” It means a limited buying window with its own access rules. Check the code or membership requirement, purchase limit, delivery timing, and refund terms before you rush to checkout.

## Venue-guide post

The section name is only the beginning. A venue guide should answer: where is the section, what is the view like, which gate is closest, and what changes for concerts versus games? Check those details before choosing a seat.
