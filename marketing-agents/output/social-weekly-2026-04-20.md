## 8. Voice / Style Guardrails (reminder for executing posts)

From accumulated feedback across the daily reports:

- **Banned words / phrases:** "delve", "crucial", "pivotal", "stands as", "testament", "here's the thing", "not just X; it's Y", "here's what you need to know", "no guessing", "no wasted clicks".
- **No em dashes for emphasis** in post copy (use colons / periods).
- **Varied sentence length** -- short fragments + longer conversational lines in same post.
- **Specific dollar amounts / sections / venues / sources** in every data post.
- **First person** is allowed and encouraged ("I pulled...", "we ran..."). Natural, not corporate.
- **No generic CTAs** ("compare every platform in seconds"). Ends of posts should be URLs or natural closers, not marketing.
- **Instagram:** 5 hashtags max, 0 emojis in caption body.
- **TikTok:** hashtags can include emojis sparingly, caption body emoji-free.
- **No rule-of-three forced groupings, no "-ing" tacked-on phrases, no "the future looks bright" wrap-ups.**
