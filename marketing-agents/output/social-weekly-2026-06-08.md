## 3. Hero Content Piece — Twitter/X Thread

**"Two World Cups are happening this week. One starts at $116. One costs $33,000."**
*Ships Tuesday 6/9. Anchored entirely on press figures already in hand — no blog dependency. Adapts to an 8-slide IG carousel (same copy, one figure per slide).*

> **1/** Two World Cups are happening at the same tournament this week.
> One of them, you can get into for about $116. The other will run you $33,000.
> Here's the buy-or-wait read on every seat tier before Thursday's kickoff. 🧵

> **2/** The cheap one first.
> Neutral group-stage fixtures have been *panic-sold* down to roughly $116–$120 on resale: Ivory Coast–Curacao from ~$116, Qatar–Switzerland and Czech Republic–South Africa from ~$120 (Goal.com).
> Our take: these have already bottomed. If you want in, buy.

> **3/** Why won't they drop further?
> The sellers who needed out are already out. Once the tournament is actually rolling, neutral-fixture supply tightens. The floor on these is about as low as it gets. This is the one tier where "wait" stops paying you.

> **4/** Now the expensive one.
> FIFA *tripled* the best-available price on the final to $33,000 (ESPN). Category-1 face value for the semis and final runs $1,500–$6,730 (Goal.com).
> That's not a market price. That's a FOMO tax.

> **5/** The honest read on the top tier: skip or wait.
> Knockout and marquee seats are either still falling or priced on hype, not demand. There's no information edge in paying the peak the week before. If you must be there, set a target and watch — don't chase.

> **6/** And the big middle.
> General group-stage resale is averaging $650–$1,100 (Goal.com). This is where comparing platforms actually moves money — the spread between resale sites on the *same seat* is widest right here.

> **7/** So, one framework:
> • Neutral group-stage (~$116+) → **buy**, the floor is in
> • General group-stage ($650–$1,100) → **compare hard**, the spread is the savings
> • Knockout / marquee ($1,500–$33K) → **wait or skip**, that's FOMO not value

> **8/** Same tournament. Same week. A 280x price gap between the cheapest and the priciest seat.
> Our only job is to show you every platform's price on the seat *you* actually want, the moment you're ready to buy.
> Compare before Thursday → ticketscan.io/world-cup-2026
