## 3. Hero Content Piece — Twitter/X Thread

**"This week, the same World Cup sends ticket prices in two opposite directions. Here's how to be on the right side of it."**
*Ships Tuesday 6/23. Anchored entirely on press figures pulled this morning — no blog dependency. Adapts to a 7-slide IG carousel (same copy, one idea per slide).*

> **1/** The group stage ends this week, and the Round of 32 opens this weekend.
> That flip does something strange: it sends ticket prices in two opposite directions at the same time.
> Here's how to be on the right side of it before the weekend. 🧵

> **2/** Direction one: group-stage seats are falling.
> Over half of group-stage matches have already seen resale prices drop 20% or more (Yahoo Sports). Standard group-stage resale has been sitting around $650–$1,100 (Goal.com), and that band only softens as matches finish.

> **3/** Why they fall: a ticket is a perishable good.
> The moment a team is eliminated, its fans' seats all hit resale at once — and once a match kicks off, an unsold seat is worth nothing. Sellers cut rather than eat a total loss. The floor drops the day the standings lock.

> **4/** Direction two: knockout seats are firming up.
> One Round-of-32 listing at Levi's jumped about 136% to roughly $1,906 — with the teams still to be decided (per reporting). Knockout supply is scarcer and demand is certain, so the premium forms *early*, before you even know the match-up.

> **5/** The honest read, by tier:
> • Group-stage seat you still want → you can usually **wait**, the floor is still dropping
> • Surviving-team knockout seat → **don't wait for it to get cheaper**, it likely won't; set your number and compare now
> • Marquee / host-nation match → that's a FOMO premium, not a value play

> **6/** The cheapest door into the knockout bracket isn't where people assume.
> Round-of-32 get-in has been reported from about $385 up to ~$798 depending on the venue (per reporting), and 13 matches had a get-in under $300 before the tournament even started (Goal.com). The bracket has bargains — they're just not the famous matches.

> **7/** One job, both directions:
> We don't sell the ticket. We show you every platform's price on the seat *you* actually want, the moment you're ready — whether you're waiting out a group-stage drop or catching a knockout seat before it firms.
> Compare before the weekend → ticketscan.io/world-cup-2026
