- Specific numbers and equivalent-seat framing are the strongest click hypotheses.

- Repeated fee math needs new examples or formats; do not recycle the same $30.07 example every day.
- TicketScan’s latest price-history record is stale after July 24. No post this week should claim a current drop, current cheapest source, alert hit, or guaranteed savings.

## 3. Competitor social intelligence

This is public positioning intelligence, not connected post-level engagement data.

### SeatGeek

Publicly visible messaging emphasizes event discovery, buyer guarantee, seat-view previews, upfront pricing, and “best deals” framing. Its Instagram search result also uses event-led content, including reaction to NBA Opening Day matchups. Sources: [SeatGeek Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/seatgeek/?hl=en), [SeatGeek August tickets](https://seatgeek.com/august-tickets), [SeatGeek newsroom](https://seatgeek.com/enterprise/newsroom).

**Differentiate:** do not compete on another marketplace’s own inventory. Own the neutral comparison layer: same event, equivalent seat, final total, and timestamp across sources.

### StubHub

The visible public conversation is currently trust- and fulfillment-heavy around World Cup resale tickets, including reports of canceled or undelivered tickets and StubHub’s buyer-support responses. Sources: [StubHub World Cup FAQ](https://support.stubhub.com/articles/61000310232-2026-soccer-world-cup-faq), [The Guardian report](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jun/27/stubhub-world-cup-resale-ticket).

**Differentiate:** publish practical buyer-protection education—delivery timing, transfer restrictions, seller notes, and what “guaranteed” does and does not mean. Do not pile on or imply every StubHub order has a problem.

### Ticketmaster

Ticketmaster is leaning on official event inventory, onsale windows, fan support, and ticket-deal promotions. Its public deal page advertises savings on two-ticket purchases, while its US Open page highlights official tournament inventory and Aug 25–26 mixed-doubles sessions. Sources: [Ticketmaster ticket deals](https://www.ticketmaster.com/ticketdeals), [Ticketmaster US Open](https://www.ticketmaster.com/usopentennis), [Ticketmaster Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ticketmaster/?hl=en).

**Differentiate:** position TicketScan as the neutral second screen: check official inventory first, then compare equivalent available listings and the final total. Official does not automatically mean cheapest; resale does not automatically mean better.

### Response opportunities

- Make “same event / same seat area / same quantity / same total / timestamp” the recurring visual grammar.
- Counter FOMO with a ceiling-first rule and conditional language.
- Use US Open session choice as the timely series: qualifying, mixed doubles, day/evening, and main draw.
- Turn fulfillment anxiety into a buyer checklist rather than a competitor attack.
