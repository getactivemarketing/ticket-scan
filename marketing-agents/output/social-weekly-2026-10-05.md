## 3. Competitor social intelligence

This is public positioning intelligence, not post-level performance data.

### SeatGeek

SeatGeek’s current X feed is event-led and visual: recent posts highlight Harry Styles seat views at Madison Square Garden, MLB postseason availability, and a Chicago Cubs/White Sox audience angle. Its public Instagram result positions the account around seats and event discovery. Sources: [SeatGeek on X](https://x.com/SeatGeek), [SeatGeek on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/seatgeek/?hl=en), [SeatGeek concert discovery](https://seatgeek.com/concert-tickets).

**Opportunity:** TicketScan should own the neutral preparation layer around the event: confirm the exact date, section, quantity, delivery terms, venue rules, and final total before a buyer chooses a seller. Avoid copying SeatGeek’s seat-view or buyer-guarantee positioning.

### StubHub

StubHub’s X feed is short, reactive, and culture-led, with posts built around pop-tour urgency, sports personalities, and jokes rather than detailed education. The public profile also emphasizes “nothing beats being there” and routes support to @TeamStubHub. Sources: [StubHub on X](https://x.com/StubHub), [StubHub on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/stubhub/?hl=en), [StubHub on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/stubhub).

**Opportunity:** Counter the urgency with calm, practical buyer protection: delivery timing, transfer restrictions, event changes, and the complete order summary. Keep the tone lightly irreverent without attacking a competitor or implying that every resale order is risky.

### Ticketmaster

Ticketmaster’s current X feed combines artist presale promotion with practical ticket education. A recent Carly Rae Jepsen post leads with the artist announcement and a precise presale deadline; another recent post links to a five-tip college-football ticket guide. Sources: [Ticketmaster on X](https://x.com/Ticketmaster), [Ticketmaster on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ticketmaster/?hl=en), [Ticketmaster concerts](https://www.ticketmaster.com/discover/concerts).

**Opportunity:** TicketScan can be the second screen that helps a fan understand the event and sale window, then review comparable options and seller terms without pretending to be the primary ticket issuer.
