Sources: [StubHub on X](https://x.com/StubHub), [StubHub newsroom](https://newsroom.stubhub.com/)

### Ticketmaster

Ticketmaster’s recent visible X posts emphasize event/onsale details, artist presales, and college-football ticket tips. Its voice is official and calendar-led.

**Opportunity:** be the second-screen explainer: translate presale/onsale labels, local time, venue logistics, and seller terms into a short checklist without presenting TicketScan as the primary ticket issuer.

Source: [Ticketmaster on X](https://x.com/Ticketmaster)
