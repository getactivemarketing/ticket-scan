## 4. Competitor social intelligence

This is public positioning intelligence, not competitor performance data. Direct X profile fetches were blocked by X, so no engagement counts are reported.

### SeatGeek

SeatGeek’s current editorial surface is event-led: tour announcements, presale guides, venue guides, fan-experience pieces, and timely sports stories. Its press page also highlights Ask SeatGeek and TourIQ, so product-led discovery and predictive language are already occupied territory.

**Opportunity:** own the neutral preparation layer: exact event identity, venue context, delivery, seller terms, and a final-total checklist. Do not imitate SeatGeek’s predictive-pricing positioning while TicketScan’s price ingestion is down.

Sources: [SeatGeek Blog](https://seatgeek.com/blog), [SeatGeek press page](https://seatgeek.com/press)

### StubHub

StubHub’s newsroom is pushing partnership/news content, NHL season framing, event guides, fees/pricing education, seat-view technology, and fall concert planning.

**Opportunity:** counter marketplace-led urgency with calm verification. A recurring “same event / same venue / same quantity / same delivery / same final total” visual would be distinct and product-safe.

Source: [StubHub Newsroom](https://newsroom.stubhub.com/)

### Ticketmaster

Ticketmaster’s public help content is calendar- and access-led: presale eligibility, ticket limits, local venue time, accessible-ticket filters, and mobile-ticket instructions.

**Opportunity:** be the second-screen explainer. Translate presale and onsale labels into a short checklist, then send users to the official event/seller page for the final terms.

Source: [Ticketmaster Help](https://help.ticketmaster.com/hc/en-us/)

### Competitive response opportunities

1. Make **event identity before price** the recurring point of view.
2. Use verified venue facts as the save/share hook: section map, entry, capacity, and event setup.
3. Reply to official presale and schedule announcements with practical preparation, not a competing sales claim.
4. Keep one “pause before checkout” asset in rotation to counter urgency-led marketplace creative.
